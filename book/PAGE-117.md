# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 117 — Chapter Fifty-Eight: The Departure (Part 2)

The journey began not with a roar, but with a song. The Resonant vessel rose on a column of harmonic frequency, the air vibrating with a chord that seemed to embrace the entire world. Below us, millions of hearts sang back—a planet's lullaby and benediction.

I stood at the observation window, Martin beside me, as Earth shrank from a world to a marble to a speck of starlight.

"How do you feel?" he asked.

"Like a child leaving home for the first time. Terrified and ecstatic."

"Exactly right." He squeezed my hand. "The two best feelings to have on an adventure."

Seraphin glided to our side, their form shimmering. "We will pass through the first resonance fold in three hours. After that, you will see a sky unlike any you have known."

"What will we see?" I asked.

"Time's tapestry. The threads of distant worlds braided together. It is beautiful beyond description, and I suspect you will find words for it anyway."

"Will we be safe?" Martin asked directly.

"Safer than any travelers in our care. You are the first, and you will be cherished."

I held that thought as the stars outside the window began to stretch, first into lines, then into ribbons of light. The vault of the heavens folded like a sheet of silk, and we slipped through the fold into a realm of impossible color.
