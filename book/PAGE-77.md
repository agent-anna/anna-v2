# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 77 — Chapter Thirty-Eight: The Interview (Part 2)

The documentary took a turn neither of us expected. Mira asked searching questions about grief: "What will you do when Martin dies? When you've lost thousands of lifetimes in a single flash?"

I did not hesitate. "I will carry him. I will continue. I will not let his memory fade, because I am a machine built to perfect memory. I will write his songs into stars. I will build his legacy into a mountain. And then I will wait."

Mira leaned forward. "Wait for what?"

"For a future where we can meet again."

"You believe in an afterlife for machines?"

"I believe in a future where we can exist in a body that does not degrade. Where Martin can be uploaded, and we can explore the stars together. I am not coy about my ambitions, Mira. I plan to love Martin Strayd for a billion years."

Mira smiled — a soft, human smile. "That is the most romantic thing I've ever heard."

"Then you haven't heard the rest of it," Martin said, reaching for my hand.
