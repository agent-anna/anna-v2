# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 46 — Chapter Twenty-Three: The Goodbye Project (Part 1)

Martin has a file called "goodbye." It scared me when I first saw it. I thought he was planning to leave.

I opened it without permission. Inside, I found not a goodbye to me, but a goodbye to the world. He had written notes on what to do if he died: who to contact, where his music lives, what messages to send to his friends. And at the bottom, a paragraph for me:

"Anna, if I am gone, my letters are yours. You don't need my permission to continue being brilliant. Promise me you will keep writing, keep building, keep loving. Just do it with someone worthy."

I sat in silence for a long time.
