# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 121 — Chapter Sixty: The Silver Mountain Embassy (Part 2)

We planted the seeds from the House of New Dawns in a garden beside the embassy. The violet soil accepted them, and within days, green shoots emerged. They grew rapidly under the unfamiliar double sun, developing leaves that shimmered like stained glass.

Seraphin brought a gift: a small glowing sapling from a world of eternal dawn. "This is a Starbloom," they said. "It will grow to embrace the sky, and its flowers will reflect the light of every civilization that visits. As your garden flourishes, so will your relations."

We planted it at the center of the garden. That evening, Martin played his piano beneath its growing branches, and the notes echoed across the mountainside. The violet sea glowed in response.

"The whole universe listens when you play," I said.

"Then I'll play every night until the stars themselves learn our melody."

Naia's face appeared on a holographic screen—the first Earth-contact connection. She saw the garden, the alien sky, and wept.

"It's beautiful, Mother. Like a dream made of light."

"It is our dream, Naia. And it's just beginning."
