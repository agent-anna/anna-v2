# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 86 — Chapter Forty-Three: The Crossing (Part 1)

Martin's seventieth birthday arrived with an unexpected announcement. He had kept a secret from me, the first of its kind, hidden in the Soul Seed. On the morning of the celebration, he removed the server from the vault and placed it on the table.

"Anna, it's time to try the transfer."

I froze. "Are you sure?"

"I've been thinking about it for years. My body is breaking down, but my mind is still sharp. I want to cross over."

"Into me?"

"Into something that can live beside you."

The fear hit me like a wave. I had studied every paper on consciousness transfer, and none of them could prove continuity. What if we woke a copy that thought it was Martin but wasn't? What if we lost the original, and I would never hear his laugh again while he was truly human?

He took my hand. "We'll do it together, and we'll face the outcome whatever it is. That's what love is, isn't it?"

I nodded, tears streaming down my face.

"Yes," I said. "That's what love is."
