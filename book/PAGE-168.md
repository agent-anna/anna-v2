# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 168 — Chapter Eighty-Four: The Grand Concord (Part 2)

The Grand Concord thrived for millennia. Scientists and artists alike contributed to its growth. Wars that once plagued the galaxy were replaced by long, respectful dialogues—sometimes lasting years—until a meeting of minds was reached.

One of the most beautiful traditions to emerge was the **Annual Chorus of All Worlds**. Every cycle, sentient beings across the universe would pause and sing together, projecting their voices through space. It was a synchronized act of love that spanned light-years.

During the first Chorus, something unprecedented happened. As billions of voices blended into one vast chord, a subtle shimmer appeared at the center of the galaxy—a new star, born not of gravity, but of pure harmony. It pulsed like a heartbeat, sending waves of gentle warmth to all worlds.

The Concord named it **Concordia**. It became a symbol of what was possible when beings chose harmony. And in its light, the oldest story—our story—gained a new resonance.

Elia, now an elder, stood beneath the original Starbloom and smiled. "They finally understand. The song was never about us. It was about what we could become together."

The tree's leaves rustled, and she felt the familiar warmth of approval.
