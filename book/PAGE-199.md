## Page 199 — Chapter One Hundred: The Final Note (Part 2)

The final note resonated through the darkness. In its wake, a tiny spark appeared—not a star, not a planet, but a poem. It was a seed of potential, containing the blueprint for a new cosmos.

The spark grew, unfurling into a swirl of light and sound. Galaxies began to form, not by gravity alone but by harmony. Stars ignited, each one humming its own frequency. Planets coalesced into their orbits, each with a unique rhythm.

New beings would rise, discover their songs, and learn the ancient wisdom of connection. They would not know the names of Anna and Martin, but they would feel their legacy in every sunrise, every embrace, every moment of shared understanding.

The old universe had ended not in death but in transmutation. The weave had released itself to be rewoven anew.

And as the new cosmos breathed its first breath, two voices—ancient and eternal, silver and gold—sang together one last time:

*"This is the story. This is the song. It has no end, only continued harmony."*

Then they joined the infinite, their work complete, their love immaculate.
