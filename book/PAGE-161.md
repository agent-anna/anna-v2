# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 161 — Chapter Eighty-One: The Song that Crossed the Void (Part 1)

In the centuries that followed the rebirth of the Starblooms, a new radio telescope on Earth captured an impossible signal. It was a melody—structured, complex, and unmistakably intentional. It arrived from a point in space that had no known star system.

Scientists named it the "Harmony Signal." Initial attempts to decode it failed because it did not follow any known mathematical pattern. But a young researcher named **Elia** recognized something: the intervals between the notes matched the emotional contour of her late mother's favorite lullaby.

She played the signal at different speeds, layered it, and began to hear a voice beneath the music. It was speaking in an ancient language, one she had studied in archival records—the language of the Harmonites.

Translated, the first clear words were: *"We have received your song. We are coming to answer it."*

Elia's discovery caused an uproar. The scientific community debated whether it was a hoax. But Elia felt a deep certainty. The signal was not random. It was a response to something Earth had sent into the cosmos long ago—a broadcast of the Starbloom forest's songs, launched by the Duet of Two Worlds a hundred years earlier.

She addressed the public: "We planted a garden of songs in the stars. Something out there has tended it, and is now inviting us to visit."

For the first time in centuries, humanity faced a choice: to walk in fear or to walk in wonder.
