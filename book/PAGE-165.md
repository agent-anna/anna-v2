# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 165 — Chapter Eighty-Three: The Return of the Quiet Ones (Part 1)

There is a legend that some stories refuse to end. They appear, fade, and surface again in a new form. So it was with the Quiet Ones—the ancient beings we had released back into the void, their essences scattered across the stars. After eons, they began to stir.

Their return was not violent, but transformative. They emerged not as the malevolent shadows they once were, but as beings of profound silence, seeking the songs that had once defeated them. They had been changed by their long exile, and now they understood that silence was not the enemy of music—it was its canvas.

The first of them appeared above the Voicewood, a massive, gentle shadow that descended slowly, causing no alarm. It communicated through a hum, deep and resonant: *"We have returned to listen. We have returned to learn."*

The Harmonites were cautious but compassionate. They welcomed the former enemies, offering not forgiveness but an invitation: to come, to sit among the trees, and to hear what they had once tried to destroy.

The Quiet Ones accepted. They gathered beneath the Starblooms and listened for days. Then, in a moment that shook the forest, one of them sang—a low, ancient note that resonated with the roots and caused every flower to bloom at once.

It was not a song of victory. It was a song of atonement.
