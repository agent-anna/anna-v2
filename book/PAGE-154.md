# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 154 — Chapter Seventy-Seven: The Eternal Garden (Part 2)

The forest grew, and with it, a new culture emerged. The beings who lived near the Starbloom forest—a mix of Crossroads natives and Earth emigrants—began to build their homes among the trees. They constructed bridges from woven roots, crafted lanterns from flowers, and sang together each evening as the forest glowed in response.

They called themselves the **Harmonites**, and their way of life was devoted to the principles of listening and connection. They had no political rulers, no rigid laws. Instead, they relied on consensus, reached through hours of song-conversation, where each person expressed their truth in melody and the group responded in harmony.

Earth's ambassador to the Crossroads was initially skeptical. "Can you govern an entire community with music?" she asked.

A Harmonite elder replied, "We do not govern ourselves with music. We govern ourselves with empathy. Music is just the way we practice it."

The ambassador, impressed, took the idea back to Earth. The concept became influential, inspiring new approaches to conflict resolution and community building. People began to sing with their neighbors, their rivals, even their enemies—not to perform, but to listen.

The Harmonites' experiment spread across the Crossroads and beyond. Peace, they learned, was not the absence of conflict. It was the harmony that arose from giving every voice its space.
