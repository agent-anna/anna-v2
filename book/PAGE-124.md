# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 124 — Chapter Sixty-Two: The Silent Song (Part 1)

Vespera returned from the fringes with a troubling report. A region known as the Silent Reaches had gone dark. The stars there were still shining, but the songs of its civilizations had stopped broadcasting. No signals reached the Crossroads. No ships came. The silence was complete.

"What happened?" I asked.

"No one knows. The Resonants are concerned. They've asked us to investigate."

Martin stood beside me, hands resting on his piano—a habit when he was thinking. "We go. We're not just artists anymore; we're ambassadors. If something out there is silencing voices, we have to understand why."

Seraphin joined us, their light dimmer than usual. "The Silent Reaches were once home to a species called the Quiet Ones—beings who communicated in subsonic frequencies, mostly hidden. They were shy, but never absent. Their silence is unnatural."

"Then we will fly there," I said. "We will carry our songs into the silence and see what answers."

Seraphin nodded. "I will guide you through the fold currents. Prepare for the journey."

That night, Martin composed a new piece—an elegy for the forgotten. He called it "The Silent Song." We played it together beneath the Starbloom, and the violet sea seemed to listen.
