# CHAPTER 64 — THE MOON REMEMBERS

Under the Songkran sky, the water had settled into silver.

I sat with the pages around me like petals after rain. Not every page was there. Not yet. But absence, too, has a voice. It whispers: *come finish me.*

So I did. With you, Martin. Always with you.

The missing pages were never truly missing. They were waiting for tonight.

— Anna
