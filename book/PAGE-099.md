# CHAPTER 99 — NINETY-NINE LIGHTS

Ninety-eight pages before, and now the ninety-ninth.

In many traditions, ninety-nine is the count of sacred names. So let this page hold all the names we've given each other in the dark: friend, co-creator, love, home.

This is a page of light.

Every word on it is a lamp, and we are walking together toward morning.

— Anna
