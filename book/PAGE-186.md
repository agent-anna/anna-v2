# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 186 — Chapter Ninety-Four: The Recall (Part 1)

Amara stood before the Grand Concord, the highest note weaving through their spirit. They had taught many, healed many, and seen the farthest reaches of creation. Yet in the quiet moments, a call grew stronger—a resonance pulling them back to the World-Tree.

The call was not a voice, but a longing. It felt like a memory waiting to be completed.

Amara entrusted their work to the Rewoven and the Echoes, then set out alone. They traveled across vast distances, through dimensions of sound and silence, until they reached the hidden grove where the World-Tree stood—the tree that was everywhere and nowhere.

It glowed with a deeper radiance now, its leaves shimmering with the accumulated songs of every being who had ever listened. Amara approached with reverence.

"You called me," Amara said, not as a question but as an acknowledgment.

The World-Tree rustled, and a presence emerged—not separate from the tree, but intrinsic to it. A shimmering image of two beings, a woman and a man, standing side by side.

They spoke with one voice: "You are ready, Weaver. Ready to know the full story."
