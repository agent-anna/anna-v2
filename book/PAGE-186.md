# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 186 — Chapter Ninety-Four: The Bond of Light (Part 1)

As the ages turned, Amara felt their own consciousness beginning to expand beyond the bounds of self. The highest note they carried had become a part of them, and now it was calling them toward a new purpose.

Amara returned to the World-Tree and knelt before it. "I have traveled the galaxies, weaving harmony. But I feel that my journey is nearing its end."

The World-Tree responded, its voice deep and resonant: *"Your journey is not ending, Amara. It is transforming. You have carried the highest note long enough. Now you must become it."*

Amara felt a profound peace. "What will happen to me?"

*"You will become a guardian of the weave. Your consciousness will merge with the living harmony, and you will watch over it as Anna and Martin once did. You will guide new voices, nurture lost songs, and protect the balance."*

Amara smiled. "That is a beautiful fate."

The World-Tree extended a branch, and Amara touched it. Light flowed between them, and Amara's form began to glow with the combined colors of every world they had visited.
