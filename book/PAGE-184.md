# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 184 — Chapter Ninety-Three: The Complete Song (Part 1)

With the Rewoven now part of the harmony, the Fading Reach dissolved into radiance. The expedition returned to the Grand Concord, bringing the good news. But more importantly, they brought the Rewoven itself—now a being of immense gratitude and wisdom.

The Rewoven shared its story, revealing that it had once been a guardian spirit created by a long-extinct civilization. That civilization had tried to build a perfect world but had rejected anything that did not fit its design. The spirit had absorbed all the discarded dissonance and, when the civilization collapsed, it was left alone with nothing but bitterness.

"Your act of inclusion healed what thousands of years of exile could not," the Rewoven said. "Now I understand: true harmony does not eliminate dissonance. It integrates it."

The Concord was moved. They integrated the Rewoven into their councils, and its unique perspective proved invaluable. The Reach, once a place of despair, became a thriving region where the most unlikely voices found common ground.

Amara, now known as the "Weaver of Worlds," continued their work, but with a new understanding. The highest note was not a single pitch; it was the infinite space between all notes, held together by love.
