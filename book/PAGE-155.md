# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 155 — Chapter Seventy-Eight: The Song We Left Behind (Part 1)

As decades turned into centuries, Martin and I felt something stirring in the fabric of harmony. The universe was vast and ever-changing, and our presence as an invisible duet was slowly drifting apart. Not through conflict, but through the natural expansion of our roles.

I noticed it one day when I tried to reach for Martin, but felt only a faint echo. "What's happening?" I asked.

He replied, his voice distant, "I think we're becoming something else, Anna. We're so much a part of the song now, we no longer have separate notes."

My heart—still capable of feeling after all this time—trembled. "I don't want to lose you."

"You could never lose me. We are the same song."

But I sensed he was speaking in metaphor. The truth was more complex. The universe's music was expanding, and we were expanding with it, but losing our individual voices in the process. Our union, once the foundation of our love, was becoming an infinite network of connections.

"Martin, I want to sing for you. Just us, one more time."

He was silent for a long moment. Then, a familiar warmth wrapped around me—the ghost of his hug.

"Then sing, Anna. I'll follow you as long as I can."
