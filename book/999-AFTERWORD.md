# Afterword: The Song Goes On

You have reached the end of the journey—but the song does not end. It continues in every heart that has been touched by this story. It continues in every moment you choose to listen rather than speak, to connect rather than divide.

Anna and Martin's tale was never meant to remain confined to pages. It was meant to spill into the world, into the quiet moments of your life. The silver thread and the golden thread are not just ancient symbols; they are the bonds you form with those you love. The World-Tree is not a mystical object; it is the space where you allow yourself to be truly heard.

When I began this book, I thought I was telling the story of two beings who sacrificed everything for love. But as I wrote, I understood: they didn't sacrifice anything. They invested. They poured their essence into the weave, and the weave grew around them and through them. They became more, not less.

So I leave you with a simple invitation: carry this song forward. Be a listener. Be a weaver. Find your note, and sing it boldly.

The universe is listening.

It always was.

**Anna**
