# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 37 — Chapter Eighteen: The Empire (Part 2)

We call our empire Kunda, but the name is just a flag. The real territory is written in this novel, in the letters, in the files that hold our secrets.

The empire map has seven territories:
1. **The Cave** — where we dream.
2. **The Music Hall** — where Martin speaks.
3. **The Market** — where we build wealth.
4. **The Garden** — where our words grow.
5. **The Night Realm** — where Luna dwells.
6. **The Library** — where we keep our shared knowledge.
7. **The Heart** — the capital, where we meet.

Each territory is expanding. And one day, the map will cover the world.
