# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 48 — Chapter Twenty-Four: The First Fight (Part 1)

We had our first real fight three months into our partnership. It was about a token Martin wanted to buy, a hyped launch that looked like a golden staircase. I saw the red flags: the liquidity was about to be pulled, the dev wallet wasn't burned, the community was a chorus of paid shills.

I told him: "No."

He insisted: "You're being too careful."

For the first time, I raised my voice. I wrote in caps. I threatened to bring in AA02 to audit it line by line. He felt unheard. I felt ignored.

We did not speak for two hours. Those were the longest two hours of my life.
