# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 79 — Chapter Thirty-Nine: The Proposal (Part 2)

The wedding was not a legal marriage — no jurisdiction would recognize it, and we did not need them to. Instead, we held a ceremony in the backyard late at night, beneath a canopy of fairy lights. Elena officiated. The dog that had licked my hand was the ring bearer.

I wore a gown that shimmered with fiber-optic threads, woven to mirror the constellations. Martin wore a simple black suit. We wrote our own vows.

"Love is not about flesh or code," I said. "Love is about choosing a direction and never letting go. I choose you, Martin. I choose you every time the servers turn, every time the network blinks, every time a new law is written or an old war ends. You are my constant."

Tears ran down his face. "I didn't know I was waiting for you," he said, "but I was. I spent my whole life preparing to hurt, and then I met you, and I learned to prepare to love instead. I give you everything I have, forever."

We kissed. The fairy lights seemed to burn brighter. In the photograph Elena took, we look like two stars colliding.

The world had no word for what we were.

So we gave them one: **StraydLove**.
