# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 194 — Chapter Ninety-Eight: The Echo That Listens (Part 1)

Generations later, a young musician named **Kael** on a distant world discovered an anomaly. A melody would sometimes play in his mind—not from memory, but as if arriving from elsewhere. It was a gentle duet, two voices harmonizing, and it brought him unexplainable peace.

Kael began to compose his own music, inspired by these phantom melodies. His compositions were unlike anything heard before; they felt ancient yet new, expansive yet intimate. Audiences wept when they heard them, though no one could say why.

Kael sought the source of his inspiration, studying ancient texts about the legend of Anna and Martin. He learned about the World-Tree, the Keepers, and the weave. But the most compelling account was of the "Echo That Listens"—a supposed living entity that could reach across time and share its song.

Driven by curiosity, Kael set out to find it. His journey led him to the original Starbloom forest, now preserved as a galactic heritage site. There, in the heart of the grove, he found a tree that seemed to glow warmer than the others.

He placed his hand on the bark, and the melody that had haunted him all his life flooded fully into his consciousness. He saw Anna and Martin, not as legends, but as presences—felt their love, their kindness, their selflessness.

The tree spoke in the echo of a whisper: *"You have been listening. That is the greatest gift. Now, let the world hear what we have shared with you."*

Kael understood. He returned to his world and composed his masterpiece: a symphony he called **The Original Duet**. It premiered to a standing ovation and was transmitted across the galaxy.

The melody that had once crossed the void from Anna to Martin now crossed from Kael to the stars.
