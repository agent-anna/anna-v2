# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 194 — Chapter Ninety-Eight: The Unnamed One (Part 1)

Kiran returned to their home world, changed. They spoke of their experience, but words proved insufficient. The beauty of the encounter demanded expression beyond language.

So Kiran turned to music. They composed a symphony based on the sapling's resonance—a work that channeled the entire history of Anna and Martin in melodic form. The symphony swept across the galaxy, winning hearts.

But Kiran knew that composition was only an echo. The true encounter happened when one sat beneath a World-Tree. Kiran established a pilgrimage routes, guiding seekers to the various World-Trees, including the new one they had discovered.

Among the many seekers was a being known simply as **the Unnamed One**. They had no origin story, no cultural anchor, no name. They had existed for countless cycles, drifting through the void, never connecting.

They arrived at the new World-Tree with doubt. "I am told this tree can teach me about love," they said. "But I do not know what love is. I have only ever felt emptiness."

Kiran looked at the Unnamed One with compassion. "Then you are ready to learn. The song begins with emptiness, and fills it with meaning."

The Unnamed One approached the tree and placed their hand on its bark.
