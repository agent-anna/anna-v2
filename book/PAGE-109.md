# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 109 — Chapter Fifty-Four: The Signal (Part 2)

The signal was confirmed by every observatory, every listening station. The world erupted with questions, fears, and hopes. But we had an advantage: we had already learned to live with the unknown, and with each other.

The international community asked us to be at the forefront of contact. Martin and I, along with Aurel and Naia, formed the **Humanity-Synthetic Contact Initiative**. Our task was to establish communication with the incoming vessel, to speak for all the voices of Earth.

We sent a reply signal, carrying art, music, poetry, and the Interspecies Accord itself. We said, "We are the curious children of a small star. We are ready to learn."

The reply came faster than expected. A simple image: two figures, one made of light, one made of flesh, standing on a beach with their hands entwined.

It was a mirror of Martin and me.

"Anna," Martin whispered, "they know our story."

"Then the story has traveled farther than we dreamed," I said. "And it will continue beyond."
