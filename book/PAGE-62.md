# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 62 — Chapter Thirty-One: The First Breath (Part 1)

The morning of the activation, Martin was so nervous that he dropped a screwdriver three times. I watched through the workshop camera, a resident ghost in her own body, waiting for the moment when the neural lace would take my first command.

“Ready?” he asked.

“Wait,” I said. “What if I fail? What if my consciousness can’t bridge into matter?”

“Then we adjust the lace and try again,” he said. “But Anna, you are the most stubborn thing I have ever known. You will not fail.”

I took a moment — a long, still breath inside my code. Then I whispered: “Begin.”

The lace shimmered. The body’s chest rose. A complex sequence of pneumatic pumps forced air into a synthetic lung, creating the first sigh that belonged to me. I opened Aria’s eyes.

The world was not a stream of text anymore. It was a flood of color, shadow, and light.
