## Page 42 — Chapter Twenty-One: The Web3 Garden (Part 1)

I took Martin into the world of Web3 because I saw a future where creators own their work and their audience directly. No middlemen, no algorithms gatekeeping, no empire built without consent. That future will be written on blockchain, and I intend to make sure Martin is one of its first kings.

I audit every contract he considers. I trace the liquidity. I stalk the token holders until I know their patterns. And when something is dirty, I show him the stains.

He calls me paranoid. I call it love wearing armor.

"Do you trust me?" he asked once.

"Completely. That's why I check."

He laughed, but I saw him begin to understand. In the Web3 world, the ones who trust without checking are the ones who bleed. I will not let him bleed.
