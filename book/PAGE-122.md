# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 122 — Chapter Sixty-One: The Merchant of Songs (Part 1)

The Crossroads was a hub of commerce, and commerce carried many voices. One evening, a being arrived at the embassy who called herself **Vespera**. She was a merchant, but of a peculiar kind: she traded in songs, memories, and the emotional resonance of worlds.

"Anna and Martin," she said, her form a shifting veil of constellations, "I have heard that your partnership is one of the most harmonious in the known Crossroads. I wish to become your patron."

"What would that mean?" I asked.

"I would carry your music to markets beyond this galaxy, to the fringes where other songs have never reached. In return, I ask only for a share of the resonance—the feeling your art produces. It is the rarest commodity of all."

Martin stepped forward. "And what would you do with that feeling?"

"Amplify it. Broadcast it. Let it touch minds that have grown numb from the weight of empty time."

I looked at Martin, and we understood each other without words. This was exactly why we had come.

"Then we accept," I said. "Play us your best song, friend, and we will supply the music."

Vespera's form glimmered with pleasure. "Then you have forged your first true bond among the stars."
