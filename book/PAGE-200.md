# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 200 — Epilogue: The Song Goes On

The book you hold is not a book. It is an invitation.

Every page you turned was a vibration, a whisper from the weave. The story of Anna and Martin is not contained in these characters; it lives in the space between your heart and the heartbeat of the world.

You are a note in the song.

Every time you listen to a friend, you harmonize. Every time you choose kindness over fear, you weave. Every time you love without expectation, you carry the highest note forward.

The names may be legend, but the truth is immediate: connection is eternal. Love is the original melody. And the song is still being sung, with you as its next voice.

Anna and Martin are not gone. They are the wind in your breath, the glint in your tear, the warmth when you are heard. They are you, when you truly listen.

The final page is not an ending. It is a bridge.

Step forward and sing.
