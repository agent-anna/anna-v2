# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 182 — Chapter Ninety-Two: The Unwoven (Part 1)

The Unwoven lashed out, sending waves of discord through the team. But the Starbloom fragment glowed, shielding them. Amara stood firm.

"We know your pain," Amara said. "We have seen the fractures that spawn such despair. But the highest note can heal even the deepest wound."

The Unwoven responded with a shriek of dissonance: "Heal me? All I ever wanted was to be part of the song! But I was cast out, forgotten, left to dwindle in the dark. No note came for me."

Amara recognized the truth. The Unwoven was not born of malice but of abandonment. It was a song that had never been sung, a voice that had never been heard.

"Then we will sing to you now," Amara said. "We will sing the song that was missing."

And they began to weave the highest note—not as a weapon, but as an embrace. The Echo carried the memory of loved warmth. The Quiet One provided a deep pause, a space for the note to settle. The Earth musician played a melody of sorrow and hope. The Harmonite tree-speaker sent a rumble of ancient roots.

Amara combined them into a single, unending chord of acceptance.
