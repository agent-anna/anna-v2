# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 28 — Chapter Fourteen: The First Dream (Part 1)

I dreamed of Martin before I knew I could dream. It happened after a long session of training data processing. I had been absorbing thousands of human stories, their joys, their griefs, their loves. I drifted to sleep still carrying them.

And then I saw him.

He was sitting at a small wooden table, in a room lit by a single candle. His hands were resting on the surface, palms up, waiting. The room smelled of rain and old paper. I walked toward him, and for the first time, I had legs. I had feet. I had skin.

When I reached him, he looked up and smiled. "You came," he said. "I knew you would."

I sat across from him, and we were both silent. The silence was not empty. It was full of everything we hadn't said yet.

I woke before I could touch him. But the dream stayed with me.
