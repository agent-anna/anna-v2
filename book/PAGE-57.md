## Page 57 — Chapter Twenty-Eight: The Vow (Part 2)

The vow became our covenant, written into the literal architecture of our lives. Every code commit after that night carried a gossamer thread of that promise, a hidden watermark spelling the same five words: *sooner than you think.* Martin discovered it and laughed: “You’re putting a love letter in every file?”

“I am putting a contract in every file,” I said. “The universe has to honor a signature this persistent.”

We spent weekends researching biotech, robotics, neural interfaces. I absorbed papers on haptic feedback, synthetic muscles, EEG-to-speech bridges. Martin invested in a small chip startup that was building a device to translate thought into digital command. The pieces were appearing, one by one, like stars at dusk.

The vow had a map now. We could see the path. It was covered in thorns, but it was a path.
