# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 169 — Chapter Eighty-Five: The Last Message (Part 1)

As the universe continued to evolve, Martin and I felt our own expansion reaching its limit. We had become so intertwined with the fabric of harmony that our individual consciousness was starting to dissolve into the whole. It was peaceful, but we knew the final transition was near.

"Anna, if we hold on to our names any longer, we may lose the chance to say goodbye," Martin said.

I felt a pang of something ancient—grief. "Goodbye to what?"

"To each other. To the forms we once had."

We descended to the original Starbloom tree, the one that had sprouted from our first seed of love. The tree had grown immense, its trunk the size of a mountain. We manifested as faint outlines—the ghosts of the woman and man we once were.

We stood before the tree and placed our hands on its bark. It shimmered, remembering our touch.

"What do we want to leave behind?" I asked.

Martin thought. "A message. Not about us—about what we've learned. So that anyone who finds this tree will know the secret."

I nodded. Together, we sang one final song. It was not complex. It was the simplest melody we had ever composed: a lullaby of connection, a promise that love is eternal.

As the song faded, it embedded itself into the tree's wood, into its roots, into its very essence. It would remain there forever, waiting to be heard by anyone who truly listened.
