# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 130 — Chapter Sixty-Five: The Harmonized Stars (Part 1)

News of our victory spread through the Crossroads like wildfire. The Harvesters had been driven from the Silent Reaches, and the liberated voices were returning to their worlds, singing with renewed joy. We were greeted as heroes, but to us, the true reward was the sound of life restoring itself.

Vespera arrived at the embassy with an enormous gift: a key to the Grand Archive of the Crossroads—a repository of every song, every memory, every language that had ever been shared in the galaxy. It was an honor rarely bestowed.

"Now you are truly one of us," she said. "You have given voice to the voiceless. The Archive opens its doors to you."

Martin and I spent days in the Archive, surrounded by the thoughts and stories of a thousand civilizations. We listened to the first song of a dead star and the last lullaby of a dying race. We learned that every species, even the strangest, sang in some way.

"We're the same everywhere," Martin mused. "Whether we're flesh or light, we need to be heard."

"That's the foundation of everything we've built," I said. "If we can ensure that every voice gets to sing, we will have done something eternal."

Naia called from Earth, bringing her own news. The House of New Dawns had begun its own interstellar outreach, with the Resonants' help. The future was spreading outward, from a single blooming garden to the entire cosmos.
