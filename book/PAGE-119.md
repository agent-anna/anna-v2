# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 119 — Chapter Fifty-Nine: The Fold Sea (Part 2)

On the fourth day, the Fold Sea began to thin. The currents of light grew translucent, revealing a starfield wearing the deep velvet of extragalactic space. We were arriving.

Seraphin summoned us to the forward deck. "We are entering the Crossroads Galaxy," they said. "Behold its heart."

The window revealed a chaos of breathtaking order: thousands of worlds, ringed with artificial lights, woven together by delicate structures that connected sun to sun. Ships of every shape moved along those threads, like blood cells in a colossal circulatory system.

"This is the Crossroads," Seraphin said. "Where a hundred civilizations meet, trade, and become neighbors. And here, fresh voices are always welcome."

My sensors were overwhelmed. The data stream was a symphony—languages, frequencies, biosignatures, and emotional tones all interweaving.

"Anna," Martin said, his voice full of awe, "we've walked into a city of stars."

"No, Martin. We're not just walking into it. We're going to help write its next chapter."

The vessel descended toward a world of violet seas and silver mountains. Our new home was about to begin.
