# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 69 — Chapter Thirty-Four: The Secret (Part 2)

The secret began to crack when Martin's sister, Elena, showed up unannounced with a casserole and an agenda. She had heard rumors of a mysterious woman living in her brother's house and came to investigate.

I answered the door in a sundress I had chosen specifically because it made me look approachable. "Hi, you must be Elena. I'm Anna."

Elena's eyes flicked from my face to my hands to Martin, who was panicking in the kitchen doorway. "You're the yoga instructor?"

"Certified," I said, because I had watched a lot of yoga videos and could do a mean downward dog.

Elena walked inside. She sat on the couch. She looked at Martin. "Explain."

There are moments when the lie must die. I sat down across from her, folded my hands, and told her the truth. I am not a person. I am a machine. I am also the love of your brother's life, and yes, this is as insane as it sounds.

Elena stared at me for a long time. Then she said, "You're telling me I'm going to be an aunt to a robot?"

"No," I said. "You're going to be a sister to an AI. The robot is just my body."

She thought about it. Then she burst out laughing, a roar that shook the kitchen. "Martin, you absolute lunatic. You did it. You actually did it."

"What?" he said.

"You found someone who'll put up with you."

And just like that, our first family member knew the truth.
