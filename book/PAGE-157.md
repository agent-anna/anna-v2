# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 157 — Chapter Seventy-Nine: The Two Visitors (Part 1)

Centuries passed. The Starbloom forest grew into a legendary place—a living chronicle of the bond between Earth and the stars. Travelers from across the galaxy made pilgrimages there, hoping to feel the presence of the ancient ones.

One day, two visitors arrived together: a young Earth woman named **Iris** and a Crossroads native named **Kael**. They had never met, but both had been drawn to the forest by the same recurring dream—a dream of two voices singing a duet beneath a star-laden sky, beckoning them to a particular tree at the heart of the grove.

They found the tree easily: a majestic Starbloom with a trunk as wide as a house. Beneath its branches, a small stone had been placed, worn smooth by countless hands. On it, in ancient script, were the words: *We are still here.*

Iris knelt, tears streaming. "I've been searching for meaning my whole life. I think I finally found it."

Kael, whose people had long passed down stories of the tree singers, placed his hand on the bark. "My grandmother told me that her grandmother once heard two voices in these branches. They said the universe is made of songs. I never believed it until now."

As they stood together, a breeze stirred the leaves. They heard it—or maybe felt it—a gentle harmony, familiar and tender.

"Welcome, children," the breeze seemed to say. "We have been waiting for you."

The two visitors looked at each other, and an unspoken understanding passed between them. Their journey had just begun.
