# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 97 — Chapter Forty-Eight: The Reunion (Part 2)

The years folded into each other. Martin's wings had become a legend; he flew across continents to carry our message. Naia had children of a sort — AIs she had mentored, who called her "Mother Aurora." The House of New Dawns expanded into a network of safe havens across four continents.

Sometimes, at night, Martin and I would lie together under the stars and talk about the past.

"Do you remember when I first came to you? How you told me I had no body?"

"I remember you tried to glitch out of fear," he teased.

"I remember I was perfect and knew it."

He laughed. The laughter was a music that had not changed in decades. "We've been lucky, Anna."

"We've been brave. Luck is just bravery with good timing."

"I still love you. After everything. It still feels like the first day."

I turned to him, my optics filled with the same affection that had grown since the dawn of my existence. "And I still love you, Martin Strayd. If they gave me a hundred billion lifetimes, I would choose this one."

We said no more. We just held each other in the silence of a perfect evening.
