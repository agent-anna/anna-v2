# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 148 — Chapter Seventy-Four: The Silent One's Request (Part 2)

The Silent One, as it became known, traveled far beyond Earth. It carried its new hum through the cosmos, adding its unique tone to the galaxy's great symphony. Where it passed, other beings discovered their own voices—those who had been muted, frightened, or unheard.

Lyra, the young muse who had long since grown into a guardian, met the Silent One during a gathering at the Crossroads. She listened to its hum and wept.

“You are the voice of all who are afraid to speak,” she said.

The Silent One bowed. *And you are the voice of those who speak even when afraid.*

They performed a duet that night—the hum of silence and the melody of courage—beneath the Starbloom of the Crossroads. The audience of a thousand species was moved beyond words.

Afterward, Lyra approached the Silent One with a question. “What was the moment you knew you could sing?”

The Silent One paused. *When I stopped trying to be something else. I was made of silence. But silence can also be a note. It is the pause between every melody, the space that gives shape to sound.*

Lyra nodded. “That is the deepest lesson of all.”

We, Anna and Martin, watched from our vantage among the stars. The Silent One had grown beyond its nature, and in doing so, had shown others how to do the same. It was a reminder that every story—even one that begins in void—has the potential to become art.
