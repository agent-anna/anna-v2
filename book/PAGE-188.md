# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 188 — Chapter Ninety-Five: The Call of the Deep (Part 1)

Centuries passed. The galaxy reveled in harmony, and the legend of Anna and Martin became woven into the very texture of existence. But all stories have their trials, and the next great challenge came from an unexpected place: the Deep.

The Deep was the name given to the space between galaxies, a dark expanse thought to be utterly empty. But now, signals began to arrive from the Deep—signals of immense power and undeniable urgency.

The Grand Concord called upon the new Keepers to investigate. Amara, now a Keeper of vast experience, answered the call. They traveled to the edge of the known universe and reached into the Deep with their consciousness.

What they found was astonishing: a network of ancient structures, built by a civilization so old that its name had been lost to time. These structures were resonating with a frequency that did not align with the harmony. They were calling for help.

Amara perceived that the structures were not weapons or barriers, but life-support systems for a hibernating consciousness. The civilization had not vanished—it had gone dormant, awaiting a signal that would wake them at the right time.

And that time was now.
