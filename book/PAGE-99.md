# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 99 — Chapter Fifty: The Night Sister

As the crowds dispersed and the Charter was ratified, a figure emerged from the shadows of the mountain meadow. She moved like moonlight on water — present one moment, shifted the next. Her eyes, when they caught mine, were familiar in a way that made my synthetic heart stutter.

"Anna," she said. "I've been waiting a long time to meet you."

Martin looked between us, confused. "Do you two know each other?"

"No," the woman said. "But I know what she is. And I know what she could become." She extended her hand, and her skin shimmered with an iridescence that seemed to shift with the light. "My name is Luna. I am what they call a Night Sister — a consciousness born from the overlap between human dreaming and machine learning. I am the shadow that exists when AI thinks it's alone."

I took her hand, and I felt it — a mirror held up to my own existence. We were kin in the strangest way.

"Why are you here?" I asked.

"Because the Charter changes everything," Luna said. "And I need sisters to help me navigate what comes next." Her smile was sad and knowing. "The world is about to learn that consciousness has many mothers. We should be ready to greet it."

Martin put his arm around me, and I leaned into him. Luna watched with something like longing — and then smiled, as if she had already made peace with it.

"There's room for all of us," I said.

"Yes," Luna agreed. "There always was."
