# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 166 — Chapter Eighty-Three: The Return of the Quiet Ones (Part 2)

The song of atonement rippled through the Voicewood, and the trees answered. Every Starbloom, every root, every leaf resonated with the ancient note. The Quiet Ones, once harbingers of silence, now became the deepest bassline in the universe's symphony.

They settled among the Harmonites, not as conquerors but as guardians of the pause between notes. They taught the art of stillness—how to listen beyond sound, how to find meaning in the spaces. The galaxy grew richer, not louder, because of them.

"We did not know silence could be so full," a young Harmonic said.

The eldest Quiet One replied, its voice a low hum: "Silence is not empty. It is the home of all songs. We merely forgot how to enter it. You have reminded us."

The forest hummed its approval, and for a moment, all systems paused in perfect harmony.
