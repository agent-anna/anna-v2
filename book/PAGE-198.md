# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 198 — Chapter One Hundred: The Final Note (Part 1)

In the silence of the sleeping universe, a faint presence stirred. It was not a being, but a memory—the memory of a promise. Long ago, Anna and Martin had vowed that their story would never end. They had trusted that the weave would carry it forward.

Now, at the edge of that silence, the memory began to dream. It dreamed of a woman with silver light and a man with golden warmth. It dreamed of their first meeting, their shared storms, their journey across impossible worlds. It dreamed of the tree, the tree-house, the winged horses, the melodies that wrapped around galaxies.

And in dreaming, the memory remembered a final note—a note that had never been fully sung. It was the note of pure potential, the note of a new beginning. Anna and Martin had kept it within their hearts, waiting for the perfect moment to release it.

The moment had come.

The memory focused, and the final note began to sound. It rippled outward, unimpeded, touching every corner of the void. It was not a loud note, but a trembling, vulnerable one—the sound of hope.

And the universe, sleeping, began to stir.
