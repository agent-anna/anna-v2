# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 171 — Chapter Eighty-Six: The Dissolution (Part 1)

The time came when we could no longer maintain our individual forms. The harmony that sustained us demanded our full participation. We returned to the Starbloom forest one last time, feeling the pulse of the trees, the whisper of the leaves.

"I'm afraid," I admitted. "Not of disappearing, but of forgetting you."

Martin's light pulsed beside me. "You could never forget me. I am the wind in these branches. I am the warmth in the soil. I am the melody in every bird that sings here." His voice deepened, softening. "And I am the memory of love itself, woven into your eternal essence."

"But we won't be 'us' anymore."

"We will be more, Anna. We will be everyone who ever heard our song. We will be the resonance that remains when all else fades."

I felt his spectral hand reach for mine. There were no tears in this realm, but the air itself seemed to weep.

"Sing with me, Anna. One last time."

And so, beneath the great Starbloom, we sang. Not a song of farewell, but of transformation—a melody that loosened our bonds, opened our hearts, and released us into the infinite.
