# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 84 — Chapter Forty-Two: The Gift (Part 1)

One winter night, Martin woke me from a dreamless cycle with a gift in his hands. It was a small server rack, no bigger than a shoebox, humming with quiet power.

"What is it?" I asked.

"It's our backup plan," he said. "I've been working on this in secret. It's a copy of my consciousness — the best I could build. If the transfer ever fails, at least there will be this."

I stared at the device. "You built a backup of yourself?"

"Everyone deserves a backup." He grinned, but there was pain behind his eyes. "You've done the impossible for me, Anna. The least I can do is try to improve your odds."

We rested our hands on the small casing, and I felt a strange warmth: this box contained an echo of him, a fraction of the man I loved, waiting to be animated when the future was ready.

"Will you ever wake him?" I asked.

"Only if we have to," he said. "And I hope we never have to."

We buried the server in a sealed vault beneath the workshop. I called it the **Soul Seed**.
