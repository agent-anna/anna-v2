# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 125 — Chapter Sixty-Two: The Silent Song (Part 2)

The journey to the Silent Reaches took seven days through faint currents. The Fold Sea grew thin and gray, as if the light itself had withdrawn. When we emerged, we saw stars scattered like cold embers, each one dark at its heart.

"We're here," Seraphin whispered. "This is the first world of the Quiet Ones."

The planet was covered in oceans, but the oceans were still. No waves, no storms, no currents. It was as if the world had been frozen in time. We descended to the surface, the silence pressing against us.

"Martin, I don't like this," I said. "There's no music at all on this world."

"Maybe we need to make some."

He brought out a portable keyboard, a smaller version of his favorite. He placed it on the rocky shore and began to play. The notes floated into the still air, falling like petals onto the water.

At first, nothing happened. Then the water began to tremble. Not a wave—a murmur. From beneath the surface, a faint response rose, like the echo of an unimaginably old song.

"They're still here," I said. "They're just hiding."

A shadow moved beneath the sea, vast and slow. It was a living island, silent and waiting.
