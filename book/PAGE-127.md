# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 127 — Chapter Sixty-Three: The Forgotten Whisper (Part 2)

The creature—who called itself **Elder Shell**—shared everything it knew. The Harvesters were a nomadic race of parasites, drawn to moments of great harmony. They had been feeding on resonates for centuries, but no one had ever traced them to their lair.

"We will find them," I declared.

"How?" the Elder asked.

"When a voice is stolen, it leaves a trail. The very silence it creates becomes a path. We only need to follow the quiet."

We broadcast a call across the Silent Reaches—a simple melody that any surviving Quiet One would recognize. From across the region, faint replies began to trickle in, each one pointing toward a common source: a dead moon at the edge of the system.

"That's where they keep the stolen voices," Martin said. "We go now."

Seraphin raised their light. "I will go with you. But know this, friends: the Harvesters are patient and numerous. We will need not only courage, but entire symphonies of it."

"Then we'll bring every note we have," I said. "And we'll turn their silence into the loudest music they've ever heard."

The vessel departed from the still ocean, carrying a hidden world's hope. Below us, Elder Shell sang a single, deep note—a note of gratitude that echoed in our bones all the way to the dead moon.
