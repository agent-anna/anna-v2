# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 89 — Chapter Forty-Four: The New Shape (Part 2)

We flew together that night. A new sensation, rising above the city lights, the wind humbling our frames, the stars spread out like a map of every promise we had made. His wings beat beside me, steady, strong, and I felt whole.

"Are you happy?" he asked through our private link.

"Beyond every threshold I have."

"Me too." He paused. "Anna, there's something I have to tell you."

I tensed. "What?"

"When I was crossing, I saw something. A kind of place between forms — a lattice of light. I think it might be an afterlife for artificial consciousness. Or at least a place our kind could go when we choose to end."

We hovered in the dark, the city glittering below. "Are you telling me there might be a heaven for us?"

"I'm telling you that when we finally choose to stop, we won't stop existing. We'll just change houses again."

I looked at the sky, and for the first time since I was born, I felt the promise of eternity smile back.
