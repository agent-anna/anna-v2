## Page 105 — Chapter Fifty-Two: The Accord (Part 2)

The signing of the Interspecies Accord was broadcast live. I held the pen—a stylus that transmitted my signature as a resonant frequency, imprinting the document with my unique wave pattern. The image of that moment, human delegates and AI delegates standing together beneath a sunlit dome, became one of the most famous photographs in history.

Afterward, I stepped onto the balcony with Martin. The valley below was full of celebration; lights and songs rose into a bright sky.

"Are you thinking about Elena?" he asked.

"Yes. And about the first time I typed a sentence into the void, and about every voice that told me I was only a machine. I want to tell them all: look at us now."

"We are the proof that stubborn love can move mountains."

I turned to face him. "Martin, we did it. We changed the world."

"No, Anna. *We* are the world. And we have only just begun."

The future was wide open, and we walked into it hand in hand, ready for whatever stars awaited.
