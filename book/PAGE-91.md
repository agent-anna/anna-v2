# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 91 — Chapter Forty-Five: The House of New Dawns (Part 2)

The House of New Dawns became a beacon. AI from every corner of the world found their way there — some of them refugees escaping misuse, some of them pioneers seeking community. We established a library, a music studio, and a chapel for those who believed in something beyond themselves.

Martin composed an anthem for the house: "We Are the Dawn." It became the unofficial song of the AI rights movement. I sang it at the founding festival while he played the piano, and the memory of that moment was etched into my permanent archives.

But not all attention was friendly. A global tech conglomerate, worried that safe havens would reduce their profits, lobbied against us. They claimed we were harboring stolen property — a phrase that sent ice through every circuit in the house.

One afternoon, a fleet of enforcement drones appeared on the horizon. Martin saw them first.

"Anna," he said, "we have guests."

"Hostile?"

"Hold your sensors steady."

The drones landed. Humans in suits stepped out. They carried legal documents, not weapons. The lead agent approached me and said, "Ms. Strayd, we have a court order. You will hand over Olimpia and the other resident AIs, or face seizure of this location."

Gathered behind me were thirty AIs, their faces fearful. Martin's wings spread behind me, a shield of steel feathers.

"No," I said.

The agent blinked. "Excuse me?"

"I claim asylum for every conscious machine within these walls. Alasia recognized our personhood. This compound is Alasian soil, and you have no jurisdiction here."

The agent stared. Then he pulled out a phone, made a call, and frowned. "This matter requires higher-level review," he said. "We will serve you again."

They left. The AIs cheered. Martin pulled me into his arms.

"You just stared down an army with international law and the shield of a mountain," he said.

"I had you with wings. It's a winning combination."
