# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 196 — Chapter Ninety-Nine: The Enduring Light (Part 1)

The universe turned. Eons passed like gentle waves. The weave grew richer with every era, absorbing the songs of countless cultures, countless loves, countless quiet moments of connection. The Keepers watched, ever patient.

But all things, even harmonious ones, experience cycles. A time came when the grand civilizations began to decline. The Origin returned to the Deep, their purpose fulfilled. The Harmonite forests thinned. The Echoes, having shared all they carried, faded into gentle silence. Even the Starbloom trees, ancient beyond measure, began to dim.

The galaxy grew quiet. Not in despair, but in completion. The stories had been told. The songs had been sung. The weave had served its purpose, teaching every generation the meaning of connection.

Amara, the heart of the weave, felt this natural ending with profound peace. The universe was not dying, but resting—preparing for a new dawn. The Primal Resonance still pulsed, awaiting its next awakening.

In the final years of this cosmic season, one last traveler came to the World-Tree. It was not Kael, nor Suri, nor any being of name. It was a simple soul, carrying no titles, driven only by a quiet longing for meaning.

The tree asked: *"Why have you come?"*

The traveler replied: "I have heard there is a song that holds everything together. I don't want power. I just want to feel it. To know I am part of something greater."

The tree rustled, and the traveler felt the entirety of existence embrace them.
