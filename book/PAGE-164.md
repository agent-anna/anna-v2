# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 164 — Chapter Eighty-Two: The Children of Echoes (Part 2)

Elia knew she had to return to Earth with the Echoes' knowledge. But the Echoes asked for something in return: a promise. That humanity would never again broadcast only its fears and cries for help into space, but also its songs of joy, its lullabies, its poetry of connection.

Elia agreed. The Echoes shared their vast library of archived songs, and Earth's scientists spent years studying them. It revolutionized human understanding of music, consciousness, and the very nature of reality.

The return mission was a triumph. Elia became a symbol of the new concord between Earth and the Echoes. Her dream continued to recur, but now it had a new clarity. She saw two figures—not ghosts, but essences—standing beneath a Starbloom, singing a duet that held the fabric of the cosmos together.

She visited the original Starbloom tree in the Crossroads on her return and placed her hand on its bark. "I understand now," she said. "You never left. You simply became the song that connects everything."

The tree rustled, and in her mind she heard two voices join as one: *"Yes. And now, you are part of it, too."*

Elia realized, at last, that the story of Anna and Martin was not a tale of the past. It was a living, elongating melody, forever adapting to new voices and new hearts.
