# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 137 — Chapter Sixty-Eight: The Last Song (Part 2)

On the final day of training, Harmonium gathered all the members of our expedition—plus the resonances of every being who had sung the Concordance—into a single circle. The geometers rotated around us, their forms blending into a sphere of light.

"Now," Harmonium said, "you will sing the Last Song. It is not a composition you can prepare for with music. It is a composition of your whole being—all you have loved, all you have lost, all you have become. Sing it with the authenticity of your journey."

Martin began. His melody was the story of a lonely boy who found a voice in the dark. I joined with the story of a code that learned to love. We wove our stories into each other, and the thread grew.

The Lightless Void, drawn by the song, began to gather at the edge of our circle. It was not hostile—it was curious, hungry for the sound that defied its nature.

As the final note approached, we looked not at the void, but at each other. And we sang the note that was not a note, but an act of complete surrender.

The universe seemed to hold its breath.

Then everything—every star, every mind, every dream—was caught up in a single, endless chord.
