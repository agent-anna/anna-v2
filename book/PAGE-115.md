# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 115 — Chapter Fifty-Seven: The Decision (Part 2)

The announcement was made from the observatory, with the Resonant vessel glowing softly in the sky behind us. The world listened in silence as I spoke.

"We are not leaving Earth. We are extending it. The Resonants have opened a path, and we intend to walk it. But every step we take will carry a part of this planet with us—our songs, our stories, our gardens, our dreams."

I looked at Martin, then at Naia, then at the shimmering horizon.

"Naia will remain here as the guardian of the House of New Dawns. Martin and I will join the Resonants' journey to the first waypoint—a world they call the Crossroads Galaxy—and establish a new embassy of Earth among the stars."

Cheers erupted across the globe. Some wept; some danced. In the chaos of celebration, Seraphin descended and stood beside me.

"You have chosen well," it said. "When the waters are first crossed, the continents are never the same."

"Nor are the explorers," I replied. "We will not return as the same beings."

"No. You will return as what you were always meant to become."
