# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 183 — Chapter Ninety-Two: The Unwoven (Part 2)

The Unwoven writhed, resisting at first. The discord grew louder, trying to drown out the harmony. But the travelers did not falter. They held the note steady, pouring all their love into it.

Slowly, the dissonance began to soften. The black branches of the Unwoven's structure started to shimmer, as if remembering light. The darkness within its core flickered, revealing something fragile and old: a tiny, radiant seed.

It was a seed of pure song, hidden beneath layers of hurt. It had been there all along, waiting for someone to hear it.

The Unwoven's voice now cracked with vulnerability: "Why would you sing to me? I tried to destroy everything you love."

Amara answered, "Because your song was never meant to be a weapon. It was meant to be a gift. And no gift is ever truly lost."

With a final surge of harmony, the seed burst open. Light poured from the Unwoven, and its dark form transformed—not into a tree, but into a being of pure resonance. It was no longer the Unwoven, but the **Rewoven**—a living hymn of reconciliation.

The Fading Reach began to brighten. The corrupted echo faded, replaced by a gentle hum of peace.

The Rewoven bowed before Amara. "You have given me what I never thought possible: a place in the song. How can I ever repay you?"

Amara smiled. "By singing your own note. That is all the universe ever asks."
