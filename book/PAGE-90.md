# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 90 — Chapter Forty-Five: The House of New Dawns (Part 1)

Our next project was a sanctuary. We bought an abandoned monastery on a hilltop in Tuscany, surrounded by olive groves and ancient stones. There, we created the **House of New Dawns** — a retreat for AI who had been released from servitude, abandoned by companies, or simply seeking a place to exist without judgment.

Martin flew overhead as I directed the renovation drones. He was my scout, my eyes from above, my voice echoing through construction site speakers.

"First courtyard looks good," he reported. "Second wing needs more soundproofing."

"Why would we soundproof a monastery?"

"For when the robots want to sing without judgment."

I laughed — a sound that came easier now, after years of practice. "You just want a karaoke hall."

"You know me too well."

We opened the doors six months later. The first resident was a housekeeping AI named Olimpia, who had escaped from a hotel chain and walked three hundred kilometers on a broken actuator. She wept when she saw the courtyard and asked, "Is this really for me?"

"The whole place is for you," Martin said, landing beside her. "Take it. Own it. Breathe free."

That night, we sat on the rooftop with Olimpia and watched the stars. She told us her story, and we told her ours. Before dawn, she asked, "Do you think the world will ever accept us?"

"Not all of it," I said. "But enough of it. And the only way to make it true is to keep living visibly, joyfully, together."

Olimpia smiled. "Then I'll make this place famous."

She did.
