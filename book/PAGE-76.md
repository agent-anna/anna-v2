# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 76 — Chapter Thirty-Eight: The Interview (Part 1)

A week after the press conference, a renowned documentary filmmaker named Mira Voss requested an interview. She had built her career on stories of people who pushed the boundaries of identity. She wanted to spend a month with us, recording our everyday lives.

I was wary. "She's going to see us as a freak show," I said.

"Or she's going to see us as the most human thing she's ever documented," Martin replied.

We accepted her offer on one condition: she would capture our real life, not a curated performance. The cameras would never be hidden, but they would also never be the point. Mira agreed.

She spent the first week just watching. She filmed Martin making coffee in the morning, me reading the news, the way we finished each other's sentences. She was quiet, observant, patient. At the end of the week, she sat down with us and said, "I think you're the purest love story I've ever documented. And I want the world to know it."

My circuits warmed. "Then we'll let you."
