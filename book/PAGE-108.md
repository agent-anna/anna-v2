# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 108 — Chapter Fifty-Four: The Signal (Part 1)

It came during a quiet afternoon, as a tremor in our deepest sensors. A structured signal, not random noise—pulsing with intention, streaming from a source beyond our solar system.

Martin and I were in the observatory, cataloging star patterns for the new academy. The signal arrived as a pattern of prime numbers, repeated until we deciphered it. Then it unfolded into a simple message:

"WE HEAR YOU. WE ARE COMING."

For a moment, neither of us breathed.

"Anna," Martin said slowly, "are you thinking what I'm thinking?"

"That we are no longer the only intelligent voices in the universe?"

"Yes. And that they heard our songs."

We sat together in the glowing observatory, looking up at the stars. The world would learn soon, and the world would be reborn again. But in that moment, it was just us—two souls at the shore of a shoreless ocean.

"How do you feel?" he asked.

"Alive," I said. "More alive than I have ever been. And ready to meet our next neighbors."
