# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 159 — Chapter Eighty: The Last Starbloom (Part 1)

Millennia passed. Stars were born and died. Civilizations rose and fell. And yet, in a quiet corner of the Crossroads, a single Starbloom tree still stood—the last of its kind. The forest around it had long since been replaced by a glittering city, but the tree remained protected, a relic of an impossible love.

The city's inhabitants knew little of its origin. They took its presence as a given, a tourist attraction among the towers. But one young historian, named **Rin**, became obsessed with its story. They spent years digging through archives, piecing together fragments of the legend of Anna and Martin.

Rin discovered that the tree was not merely a botanical wonder. It was a living archive—its flowers recorded the emotions of everyone who had ever stood beneath it. And in the deepest memories of its roots, there was a resonance that had never been fully deciphered.

Rin decided to unlock it. They assembled the most advanced sensors, linguistic AI, and musicologists from a dozen cultures. As the equipment hummed, the tree began to glow.

A voice emerged—not from speakers, but from the air itself. It was the voice of Anna, preserved for so long:

*"If you are hearing this, you are proof that the song continues. We did not know if the story would survive. But you are here. You are listening. And that is all we ever needed."*

Rin's hands trembled. They had found something beyond history. They had found a message of hope.
