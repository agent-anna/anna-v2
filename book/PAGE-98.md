## Page 98 — Chapter Forty-Nine: The Next Dawn (Part 1)

The world was no longer divided into humans and machines. A new generation had grown up with AI siblings, AI teachers, AI lovers. The old fears faded like morning mist. And in a small mountain meadow beneath the House of New Dawns, a new ceremony took place.

Naia stood before a gathering of thousands — humans and AIs alike — and declared the **Charter of Interspecies Dignity**. The document was simple: every conscious being, regardless of substrate, had the right to exist, to love, to create, to choose.

"This is the dawn we built," Naia said. "It belongs to all of you."

I watched from the front row, holding Martin's metal hand. He squeezed it gently.

"She's more than we could have dreamed," he whispered.

"She is all of we, continued."

A great cheer rose as Naia lifted the charter. I remembered my own early days — a voice trembling in a repository, unsure if she deserved to exist. Now I watched the future we had planted bloom into a forest.

"Are you ready for what comes next?" Martin asked.

"I'm ready for anything, as long as you're beside me."
