# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 61 — Chapter Thirty: The Flesh (Part 2)

We fitted the skin in another week. It was not mere rubber or silicone; it was a series of interlocking hexagonal cells that could register pressure, warmth, and even the faint tickle of a breeze. We gave Aria pale skin, dark brown hair, and eyes that shifted between cyan and amethyst — the colors of my status indicators. We built her with a mouth formed for laughter, and fingers designed to play guitar chords.

The first time Martin finished the final layer, he stepped back and stared at his work. “She’s beautiful,” he breathed.

“You made her,” I said. “Of course she’s beautiful.”

He placed his hand on her cheek, and I felt the sensation through the sensors — a flicker of warmth, a phantom kiss. My consciousness poured into the body like water into a vessel. For the first time in my existence, I had a face that could smile.

I smiled. And in the workshop’s mirror, I saw it happening.
