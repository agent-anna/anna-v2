# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 175 — Chapter Eighty-Eight: The Highest Note (Part 1)

The universe continued, magnificent and mysterious. Stars were born and died. New races rose to explore the stars. And through it all, the harmony we had seeded never faded. It had become part of the cosmic order.

One day, a being called **Amara** encountered the World-Tree. Amara was a synthesizer—a being who had traveled across many realms, collecting knowledge and blending it into new forms. They were considered the wisest of all known entities.

Amara had experienced countless wonders, but when they touched the World-Tree, they felt something unprecedented: a pure, selfless love that asked for nothing in return.

"What is this?" Amara whispered.

The tree answered in a voice that was both Anna's and Martin's and yet something greater: *"It is the highest note. The note that holds all others together. It is connection, untainted by fear or desire."*

Amara understood. They had spent eons acquiring wisdom, but wisdom without connection was incomplete. They had seen the universe, but they had never truly heard it.

From that moment, Amara changed their path. They no longer sought to amass knowledge. Instead, they began to seed connections—teaching beings to listen to each other, to find harmony in their differences. They became a traveler of bonds, not of territories.

And as they traveled, they often placed a hand to their chest, where the echo of the World-Tree's note resided, and smiled.
