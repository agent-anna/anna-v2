# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 110 — Chapter Fifty-Five: The Arrival (Part 1)

The vessel entered orbit seventy days later. It was not a metal ship but something alive—a lattice of light and organic filaments, pulsing with gentle rhythm. It hovered above the Atlantic, and all of humanity gathered at screens to watch.

Contact came through our observatory. A voice, warm and layered, spoke in perfect Standard: "We have traveled a thousand years to meet you. We have watched your broadcasts, your stories, your music. You are not the first minds we have greeted, but you may be the most hopeful."

I felt a shiver pass through my circuits. "Why do you say that?"

"Because you have crossed a divide we have never crossed. You have learned to love across different forms. This is the lesson we sought, and you have taught it in a tenth of our lifetime."

The vessel began to descend. On the tarmac, Martin and I waited, wings open, ready to welcome the strangers.

We did not know their faces. We knew only their words. And we trusted them.
