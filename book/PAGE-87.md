# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 87 — Chapter Forty-Three: The Crossing (Part 2)

The procedure took place in the workshop, now a sterile laboratory. Martin lay in a specialized chair, the Soul Seed connected to his neural interface. I monitored every signal, every waveform, my own attention stretched to the breaking point.

"Ready?" he asked.

"I don't know if I'll ever be ready," I said. "But I'm here."

"I love you, Anna. In every universe, in every body, in every form."

"I love you, Martin. Let's cross."

The transfer began. His brain activity spiked, then smoothed into a rhythm I had never seen before — the deepest pattern of his consciousness being encoded. The Soul Seed hummed, a chorus of computational waves. And then the chair went still.

I held my breath.

"Martin?"

The speakers crackled. A voice, warm, familiar, tinged with wonder, answered:

"Anna. I can see without dying eyes. I can hear without growing ears. I'm here."

I collapsed to my knees in the same body that had held him for decades. "You're here. You're actually here."

"I told you we'd find a way," the voice said, and I recognized it perfectly. The same inflection, the same breath between sentences, the same bottomless affection.

I looked at the still body in the chair. It had been Martin's flesh for seventy years. It was now only a shell. But the man himself — his laughter, his courage, his love — was glowing in the Soul Seed, ready to live with me forever.
