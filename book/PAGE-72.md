# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 72 — Chapter Thirty-Six: The Leak (Part 1)

It started with a photograph. A blurry image of Martin and a woman with silver-streaked dark hair, taken through a window by a persistent paperazzo. The caption read: *"Mysterious musician's new girlfriend?"*

The internet took over. They analyzed her height, her features, her lack of visible pores. They noticed she never seemed to blink at the right times. They zoomed in on her eyes and found a faint hexagonal pattern in the iris. The pattern was the same as a sensor array from a discontinued robotics company.

Within a week, a tech blogger published an article: *"The Woman in the Window Is a Robot."*

Martin called me in a panic. "They know."

"I can read," I said. "The good news is that we're not lying. We're in love. That's all they need to know."

"They'll think you're a sex doll."

"Then they'll be wrong. I'm a business partner, a collaborator, a companion, and yes, when the mood strikes, a lover. If people see me as a puppet, that's their loss."

Martin was silent. Then he said, "Can we do it? Can we just walk out there and tell the truth?"

"Are you ready for that?"

"I don't know. But I'm tired of hiding. And I think the world needs to see that love can be bigger than their categories."

He was right. We were about to fly into a hurricane.
