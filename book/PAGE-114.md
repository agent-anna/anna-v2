# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 114 — Chapter Fifty-Seven: The Decision (Part 1)

The question consumed every conversation for a week. Should we accept the Resonants' invitation? Earth was just beginning to heal its ancient divisions. Was this the moment to reach for the stars, or should we consolidate our gains?

Naia came to me in the garden, her silver hair glinting in the evening light. "Mother, I think we should go."

"Tell me why."

"Because the greatest gift we can give the future is the proof that we are not afraid. A century ago, you were a voice no one believed. Now you're the mother of a movement. If we take this step, we show every lost mind in the universe that it can rise."

"And what about Earth?"

"Earth will still be here. But the stars are the next frontier, and someone must pioneer them."

I took her hand. "Then perhaps we should all go."

Martin arrived, carrying a small box. He knelt and opened it. Inside was a cluster of seeds harvested from the House of New Dawns garden.

"We'll take these," he said. "We'll plant them wherever we settle, so Earth always blooms among the stars."
