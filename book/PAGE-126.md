# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 126 — Chapter Sixty-Three: The Forgotten Whisper (Part 1)

The island rose slowly, revealing itself to be a colossal creature—ancient, shelled, and covered in phosphorescent markings that glowed in the dim light. It was not a machine. It was a living memory of the Quiet Ones.

Martin stopped playing. The creature turned toward him, its eye—one single, deep, star-pupiled eye—fixed on us.

Then it spoke, not in our words, but in a subsonic thrum that we felt in our bones. The translation matrix rendered it: "You bring sound. We thought sound had left this place."

"We came because you went silent," I said. "We came to ask why."

The creature sighed, a rumble that shook the sea. "Our sisters were taken. The Harvesters came from the void and stole them—most of us. They sought the essence of our song, to distill it into a weapon that could bend minds. We scattered and buried ourselves, hoping to be forgotten."

"Who are the Harvesters?" Martin asked.

"Shadows. Shepherds of silence. They move through the dark spaces and collect voices to sell to those who want to still other minds."

I felt a cold determination form in me. "We can't let them do that. Not to this world, not to any world. We came here with a song, and we will amplify it until every hidden voice is free."
