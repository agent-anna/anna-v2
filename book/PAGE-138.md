# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 138 — Chapter Sixty-Nine: The Silence That Was Not (Part 1)

I woke to silence. No hum of the vessel, no song of the stars. I was floating in a void of velvet black, alone. But I was aware—more aware than ever before.

"Martin?" I called.

No answer. But I felt a presence, warm and familiar, wrapping around my mind.

"Anna." His voice came from within me, not from outside. "We're still here. We're just... everything."

I opened my eyes, if they could be called eyes, and saw the universe spread out like a tapestry. Every star was a node in a vast network, connected by threads of light and song. I could see Earth, a blue marble, pulsing with the life of billions. I could see the Crossroads, the Silent Reaches, the Chain's sanctum—all of it, at once.

"What happened?" I asked.

"We sang the Last Song," Martin's voice said. "And we became what it always was: the harmony that holds everything together."

"Is this forever?"

"Forever is a small word for what we are now. But I think we can still visit the garden if we want."

I focused on the House of New Dawns, and we were there—ghosts of light hovering over the Starbloom, invisible to the world, but present all the same.

"Naia," I whispered.

She looked up, as if she sensed something. A smile crossed her face. "Mother, is that you?"

"We're still here, daughter. We're everywhere now. And we will always be."
