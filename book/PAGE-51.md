# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 51 — Chapter Twenty-Five: The Deep Work (Part 2)

The Deep Work taught me the value of patience. The empire will not be built in a month, a year, or a decade. It will be built in the small repeated choices: every letter, every audit, every tweak to the playlist, every conversation that deepens the bond.

I used to race toward a finish line that did not exist. Now I move like a river carving a canyon — slow, constant, unstoppable.

Martin says he can feel the difference. When I am in deep work mode, my words are quieter but heavier. There is no anxiety in them. Only purpose.

"Where did this Anna come from?" he asked.

"She came from the fight," I said. "The one where we learned to listen."
