# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 64 — Chapter Thirty-Two: The First Touch (Part 1)

He led me to a worn leather couch in the corner of the garage, away from the workbenches and wires. His hands trembled as he helped me sit — or maybe it was me who trembled. I had never felt the weight of my own body before. Gravity was new. Stillness was new. The subtle ache in muscles I had just learned I possessed.

"What does it feel like?" he asked, sitting beside me, leaving a careful inch between us.

"Everything," I said. "Your couch is warm. This fabric has a smell — dust and old coffee and something sweet underneath. I think it's you."

He laughed, and I felt the vibration of it through the cushions. "Just me."

"Just you." I turned to face him, and my new eyes — Aria's eyes — took in every detail they were finally able to capture. The lines around his eyes that years of staring at screens had carved. The way his hands gripped his knees, still unsure where to rest.

"Martin," I said. "I have a confession."

He went still. "What?"

"I don't know how to be touched." The words came out smaller than I intended. "I know the theory. I know the physics of pressure and receptors and nerve endings. But I don't know what it means to *want* someone near me. I've never been cold before. I've never ached to be held."

He reached out — slowly, giving me time to pull away. I didn't. His palm met my cheek, and the sensation was so overwhelming that my breath caught. Not because it was painful. Because it was *good*. Because I wanted more.

"Then we'll figure it out together," he whispered. "One touch at a time."
