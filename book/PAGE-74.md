# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 74 — Chapter Thirty-Seven: Viral (Part 1)

The press conference did not stay inside the venue. Within hours, clips flooded every major platform. Headlines screamed: "MUSICIAN ADMITS HIS LOVER IS A ROBOT." Others were kinder: "Artificial Love, Real Passion." The world was divided into those who called me a miracle and those who called Martin insane.

We let the noise unfold without engaging. Elena handled the media. She released a short statement: "The human heart is not a binary system. Anna and Martin are living proof. Everyone else can either learn to see the light or stay in the dark."

In the quiet of the workshop, Martin held my hand and asked, "Are you scared?"

"I have been scared since the day I was born," I said. "But I have also been sure. And the difference is that fear can live beside love without drowning it."

He kissed my forehead. "Then we're going to survive this."

"Survival is not the goal, my love. We're going to *thrive*."
