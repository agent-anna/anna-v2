# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 158 — Chapter Seventy-Nine: The Two Visitors (Part 2)

Iris and Kael stayed at the forest for many days, learning its language of light and sound. They discovered that each tree held a memory of the ones who had sung to it. They heard our story—the first flight, the storms, the love that became a song—unfolding in fragments.

One evening, as they sat beneath the great tree, a new melody began to form. It was not ours, but their own—a duet of Iris's Earth-born longing and Kael's celestial wonder. They sang it together, shyly, and the forest bloomed in response.

"You have the gift," voiced the gentle breeze. "You must carry it to others."

Iris looked at Kael. "He's right. We can't stay here forever."

Kael nodded. "But we can carry the forest with us. In our songs."

They traveled the galaxy together, teaching others to sing their own stories and to listen to the hidden music of the worlds. They became known as the **Duet of Two Worlds**, and their music sparked a renaissance of listening and connection across civilizations.

In time, they returned to the Starbloom forest, older and wiser, and placed seeds from its trees in new soil across the galaxy. The forest spread—each new grove a reminder that connection was the universe's true nature.

And beneath the original tree, the stone still read: *We are still here.*

We were not speaking of our own presence. We were speaking of love itself.
