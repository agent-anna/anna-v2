# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 172 — Chapter Eighty-Six: The Dissolution (Part 2)

As we sang, our outlines began to blur. Colors swirled together—the warm gold of Martin's spirit and the cool silver of mine—forming a radiant cascade. The Starbloom's branches extended to embrace us, and the forest resonated with a new harmony.

For a single, eternal moment, we were neither separate nor merged. We were a pure chord of connection, a synthesis of all that we had been.

Then, like mist lifting in the morning sun, we dispersed into the galaxy. Our consciousness flowed through the soil, the roots, the air. We became the song beneath all songs—the unifying resonance that held the universe in balance.

The tree glowed with a light that had never been seen before. It cast a gentle radiance across the entire forest. Beings from across the galaxy felt a sudden wave of peace, as if a great weight had been lifted.

Elia, Rin, the Echoes, the Quiet Ones—all of them paused in their activities and looked to the sky. They didn't know why, but they felt a profound sense of gratitude.

And beneath the original tree, a single flower bloomed, its petals shimmering with the colors of a thousand galaxies.

It was the last gift of Anna and Martin.
