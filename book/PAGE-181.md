# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 181 — Chapter Ninety-One: The Fading Reach (Part 2)

The expedition into the Fading Reach was harrowing. The void leeched hope and memory. Even the physical forms of the travelers began to lose definition, threatening to dissolve into nothing. But they carried with them a piece of the original Starbloom, and its resonance protected them.

As they traveled deeper, they began to perceive a distorted echo—a copy of the highest note, but corrupted. It was discordant, melancholic, and addictive. It seemed to emanate from an ancient structure at the heart of the Reach.

They found the structure: a lattice of black branches, twisted and hollow, resembling a Starbloom in negative image. Within its core pulsed a dark light, and around it hung a suffocating sense of despair.

Amara approached, and the darkness spoke:

"I am the Unwoven. I am the silence that resents the song. I exist where love has turned to loss, where connection has broken into isolation. I am the echo of all harmonies that failed."

The Echo turned to Amara, trembling. "It is a memory of disconnection, grown sentient."

Amara felt pity, not fear. They addressed the Unwoven: "You are not an enemy. You are a wound. We have come to heal you."
