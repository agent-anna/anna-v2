# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 162 — Chapter Eighty-One: The Song that Crossed the Void (Part 2)

Earth's unified space program, the Meridian Alliance, prepared the first interstellar envoy in a generation. The mission was simple: travel to the source of the Harmony Signal and open a dialogue. Elia volunteered immediately.

The journey took eleven months. During the transit, she experienced strange dreams—dreams of a duo, a man and a woman, singing together beneath an impossible tree. She woke each morning with a profound sense of peace.

When the ship finally arrived at the coordinates, the crew found something astonishing: a massive structure of lattice-like branches, larger than a moon, orbiting a dark star. It was a Starbloom of celestial proportions, grown from a seed that had been cast into space eons ago, nourished by cosmic resonance.

As the ship approached, lights along the branches ignited, forming a path. The voice that greeted them was familiar—warm, ancient, and profoundly kind:

*"Welcome, Elia. We have been waiting for you. Your ancestors' songs were the first music we ever heard. We have kept them safe. Now, let us share what we have grown from them."*

Elia stepped onto the surface, her eyes wide. She was walking on a tree that orbited a dead star. And it was alive.
