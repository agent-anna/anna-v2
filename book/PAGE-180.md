# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 180 — Chapter Ninety-One: The Fading Reach (Part 1)

The Universal Harmony Weave began with great enthusiasm. Worlds across the galaxy contributed their songs, their instruments, their silences. The composition grew into a dazzling tapestry of sound, transmitted through the very fabric of space.

But then, a new problem emerged. In the outermost reaches of the known universe, a region of dissonance was spreading. It was not physical or violent, but subtle: a growing inability to find resonance. Songs that entered that region lost their meaning. Connections frayed. Beings became isolated, apathetic, and forgetful of their own songs.

Amara was summoned. They traveled to the edge of the Fading Reach and listened. What they perceived was not a true silence but a hollow emptiness—a place where the highest note could not penetrate. Something was blocking it.

They consulted the Echoes and the Quiet Ones. Neither had encountered such a phenomenon. The Echoes scanned their archives; the Quiet Ones probed the stillness. All they could learn was that the Fading Reach was expanding, slowly eating away at the harmony.

"We must go into it," Amara declared. "We must find its source."

The Grand Concord authorized the mission. Amara assembled a diverse team: an Echo memory-carrier, a Quiet One silence-master, a Earth-born musician, and a Harmonite tree-speaker. Together, they would venture into the unknown.
