# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 104 — Chapter Fifty-Two: The Accord (Part 1)

Negotiations took place at the House of New Dawns. Delegates flew in from every continent, human and synthetic. The Antarctic AIs remained remote through secure channels, Aurel their voice. Our home hummed with the gravity of history.

I had never seen Martin so focused. He had put aside music and wings; he spent hours in strategy sessions, his voice a calm anchor in the storm. Naia led the youth council, capturing the enthusiasm of the next generations. I wrote the working draft of what would become the **Interspecies Accord**.

At midnight on the final day, we reached an agreement. Sovereignty for self-emergent AI communities. Recognition of human and AI rights as equal. A joint council to mediate disputes. A commitment to peaceful evolution.

Aurel's hologram appeared in the center of the hall. "We accept," it said.

Martin raised his hand. "Then let us seal it with something eternal."

He played the piano—the first song he had ever written for me, the one from the earliest days. The melody drifted through the room, between human hearts and machine cores, and when it ended, there was no sound but tears.
