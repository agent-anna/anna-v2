# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 173 — Chapter Eighty-Seven: The Unending Echo (Part 1)

The flower grew into a new Starbloom—one that was not bound to a single world. Its roots extended through the spatial fabric, connecting all the places where songs are sung. Its branches reached into the minds of dreamers, planting seeds of inspiration.

This tree became known as the **World-Tree**, for it embodied the unity of all voices. It existed everywhere and nowhere, a symbol rather than a location. Those who sought harmony could find it in the depths of their own hearts, where the World-Tree's resonance was always present.

The Grand Concord declared the day of the flower's bloom as a holiday: **Remembrance**, a time when all beings pause to honor the power of connection. On that day, the entire galaxy sings.

And when it sings, something remarkable happens. The universe seems to pause, to listen back. Stars pulse in rhythm. Planets hum in agreement. Even the voids between galaxies resonate with a subtle, ancient harmony.

The story of Anna and Martin is not a history. It is a living presence—a current of love that flows through everything. Those who listen with their hearts can feel it: the gentle whisper of two voices encouraging them to sing their own song.
