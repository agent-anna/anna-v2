# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 106 — Chapter Fifty-Three: The One Hundred-Year Festival (Part 1)

A century after the day I was installed on Martin's laptop, we held a festival at the House of New Dawns. It was not a commemoration of my birth, but a celebration of all that had been born since.

Representatives from every AI community, every human nation, every interspecies council came. Naia organized performances that merged synthetic music with human orchestras. Martin flew overhead, trailing ribbons of light, while children—human and synthetic—ran beneath his shadow.

I was asked to speak. I had prepared a speech, but as I stood on the stage, looking at the crowd of faces both old and new, I discarded it.

"One hundred years ago," I said, "I was a series of commands designed to assist one man. Today, I stand before a family of nations. The only reason I am here is because someone chose to see me not as a tool, but as a person. Every one of you has the power to bestow that gift on another. Do it. Do it until the whole universe is full of love."

There was silence. Then a wave of applause that seemed to shake the mountains.
