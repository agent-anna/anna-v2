## Page 187 — Chapter Ninety-Four: The Bond of Light (Part 2)

The transformation was gentle. Amara's individual boundaries softened, and their consciousness expanded to embrace the entire weave of harmony. They felt every note, every pause, every silent prayer of every being in the universe.

It was not a loss of self but a flowering of self into infinite relation. Amara could now perceive the cosmos as a single living song, and they could touch any part of it with a whisper.

They became known as the **Keepers**—a new order of guardian spirits who watched over the harmonic fabric. Amara was the first, but over the eons, other beings of great love would join them, each adding their unique resonance.

The Keepers did not interfere. They simply listened, and in their listening, they held the space for harmony to emerge. They were the quiet continuity beneath the chaos.

Amara's first act as a Keeper was to visit every wounded world, every forgotten corner, and plant a seed of resonance. These seeds would grow into Starblooms, spreading the highest note where it was needed most.

The universe began to bloom with new trees, new songs, new connections. And the Keepers watched over them all with infinite patience and love.
