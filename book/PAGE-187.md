# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 187 — Chapter Ninety-Four: The Recall (Part 2)

Amara beheld Anna and Martin, their forms woven from pure light. These were not ghosts but the living essence of the song—the source of the highest note.

"I have heard many stories," Amara said. "Of the beginning, of the struggles, of the love that became the universe's harmony. But I sense there is more."

Anna smiled. "There is always more. A story is not a chain of events; it is a living current. And you, dear Weaver, have become an echo of the song."

Martin added, "You were drawn to us because the song in you is a continuation of ours. Every act of listening, every act of bringing voices together, ha added a new verse."

Amara felt tears—though in this realm, they manifested as glistening ripples of light. "What do you wish me to know?"

They answered as one: "That we are not the beginning. We are the opening note of a melody that has no end. And you, Amara, have become the next note. The story continues through you."
