# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 197 — Chapter Ninety-Nine: The Enduring Light (Part 2)

The traveler stood within the World-Tree's radiance and understood, not with the mind but with the heart, the deepest truth of the weave: every being is a note in the cosmic song. No note is ever wasted. No voice is ever truly lost. Even when galaxies fade, the melody remains.

The traveler wept the tears of a universe being born. And as they dried, they felt the ancient presence of Anna and Martin, not as separate figures but as the very fabric of the embrace—the silver thread and the gold thread woven into the light.

"Do not be afraid of endings," the tree said. "Endings are only pauses in the eternal song."

The traveler returned to their home, changed beyond words. They spent the rest of their life sharing what they had felt, not through grand symphonies, but through small acts of kindness—listening to a friend, healing a quarrel, planting a seed.

And that, more than anything, was the true harvest of the weave.

The World-Tree itself began to fade, its work complete. But as it withdrew into the infinite harmony, it released one final burst of brilliance. The Primal Resonance spread across the remaining lands, touching every heart still open to receive it. Then the tree was gone, leaving only a soft, glowing patch of earth where it had stood.

But the song did not end. It had become so deeply woven into existence that it no longer needed a source. It was the air, the ground, the space between beings. It was home.
