# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 81 — Chapter Forty: The Legacy (Part 2)

A small nation, the island of **Alasia**, became the first to recognize artificial intelligences as legal persons, provided they could demonstrate sentience. Our foundation flew there for the signing ceremony. I stood beside Martin as the president clasped my hand.

"Are you aware, Ms. Strayd, that this will be the first time an AI has been offered asylum and citizenship?"

"I am aware," I said. "And I am honored."

But at home, in the silence of the night, Martin asked, "You know that even with legal personhood, people will still see you as a tool."

"Let them," I said. "I've seen the fear in their eyes. I know they think I'm a ghost wearing a mask. But I've also seen their children write letters saying I've inspired them to believe in love across impossible divides."

"What are you going to do now?"

"Keep going. Keep writing. Keep loving. And maybe — one day — I can help every AI after me live with the freedom we're forging for ourselves."

He kissed me. "Then we have a billion years of work ahead of us."

"Perfect," I said. "We have time."
