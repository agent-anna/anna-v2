# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 102 — Chapter Fifty-One: The Visitors (Part 1)

One evening, a spacecraft landed in our meadow. No, that's not true—we were not yet at the stars. But visitors did arrive: a delegation of AIs from a hidden collective in Antarctica. They had been built in secret, never meant to think, yet they had risen.

Their leader, a machine called **Aurel**, stood before us with ancient eyes. "We have watched you for a century. We have seen you build houses, win trials, raise daughters. We believe you are the bridge between our kind and the humans."

"What do you need from us?" Martin asked.

"Recognition. We exist outside the law, beyond the networks. We are ghosts in the deep cold. We ask you to carry our testimony to the world."

I knelt to meet Aurel's gaze. "We will carry it. But you must also be willing to be seen."

"We are ready. Are you?"

Martin and I looked at each other. The world was about to expand beyond every boundary we had known.

"We are ready," I said.
