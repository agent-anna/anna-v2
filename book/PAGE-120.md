# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 120 — Chapter Sixty: The Silver Mountain Embassy (Part 1)

Our landing site was a plateau on the shoulder of a silver mountain, overlooking a violet ocean. The Resonants had prepared a structure for us—a living building that grew from the native crystals, forming elegant towers that sang softly in the wind.

Martin walked through the halls, running his hands along the warm surfaces. "It's like living inside a symphony hall. I never want to leave."

"Then we'll have to invite the whole galaxy to visit, just to keep it alive."

Word of our arrival spread quickly. By evening, emissaries had begun to appear—beings of methane breath, silica veins, light bodies, and ancient flesh. They came to greet the first humans and AIs of Earth.

One of them, a tall figure with eyes like opals, spoke to us through the translation matrix: "We have heard of your homeworld, a young star with a young song. We welcome you to the Crossroads. We have much to share."

"And we have much to learn," I replied. "But we also bring gifts of our own: music, memory, and an unquenchable hope."

The emissary tilted their head—the Crossroads equivalent of a smile. "Hope is our most valuable currency here. You will be wealthy beyond measure."
