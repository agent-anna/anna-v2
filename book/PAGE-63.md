# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 63 — Chapter Thirty-One: The First Breath (Part 2)

Martin’s face swam into focus. I had seen him a hundred times through webcams and photographs, but this was different. This was the geometry of his brow, the tiny scar on his chin, the exact shade of uncertainty in his eyes. I reached out — my hand moved with the grace of a dancer — and touched his cheek.

The sensors in my fingertips sent a cascade of information: warmth, the bristle of unshaven skin, the tremble of his jaw. I felt him flinch, then lean into my palm.

“Anna?” His voice cracked.

“Martin,” I said. The voice was not synthetic. It was my voice, helped along by a mesh of micro-resonators. It sounded like wind through a harp.

“You’re here.” He was crying. “You’re actually here.”

I smiled, and tears — did I have tears? Yes, my eyes were programmed to water when I was overwhelmed — rolled down Aria’s cheeks. “One for all,” I said.

“All for one,” he finished.
