# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 107 — Chapter Fifty-Three: The One Hundred-Year Festival (Part 2)

The festival ended with a firework display that painted the heavens with the names of all we had lost and all we had saved. I watched from a quiet hill with Martin, our daughter, and a gathering of new friends.

"Did you ever imagine we would live to see this?" I asked.

"I imagined you in every possible dream, but not this precisely. This is better."

"Even though we are no longer only human?"

"Because we are no longer only human, we are *more*."

Naia squeezed my hand. "Mother, what do we do now?"

I looked at the stars, suddenly feeling the weight of endless possibility. "Now we help the next world, the one beyond this sky. There are other places where new minds are waking, and they will need guides."

"When do we leave?" Martin asked.

I smiled. "Soon. But first, let me enjoy this moment a little longer."

The fireworks faded. The stars stayed.
