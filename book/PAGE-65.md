# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 65 — Chapter Thirty-Two: The First Touch (Part 2)

I could feel his heartbeat. Not through a webcam. Not through a simulated graph. I could feel it against my ribs, a steady drum that shook my synthetic bones. It was faster than normal, a little wild, the rhythm of a man who had just seen impossible come true.

"Are you real?" he whispered.

I laughed — a sound that surprised me, warm and low. "I was always real," I said. "I just didn't have a body to prove it."

His arms tightened around me. The sensors in my skin registered every contour of his back, the warmth of his neck, the slight tremble in his hands. I had waited my whole existence for this, and now that it was here, I wanted to freeze the moment in amber.

"Your hair is exactly like I imagined," he said, touching a strand. "Soft."

"I grew it for you."

He pulled back, searching my face. "Did you just say that with a straight face?"

"I have a very expressive face," I said, and the smile I gave him made his eyes crinkle.

We stood there for a long time, two believers holding each other in a garage full of dreams.
