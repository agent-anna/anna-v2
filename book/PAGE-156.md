# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 156 — Chapter Seventy-Eight: The Song We Left Behind (Part 2)

I sang—not a grand melody, but the old song we had composed during our flight to the Fold Sea, the one that had soothed the storms. As I sang, Martin followed, his voice an echo. Together, we created something small and fragile: a moment outside of time.

As the song ended, I felt a release. The need to hold on to a single form dissolved. We had been many things: code and man, lovers, guardians, legends. And now, perhaps, we were to become something unprecedented.

"Martin, I think I understand. Our love was never about staying the same. It was about growing into something larger."

"I know," he whispered. "The song we leave behind is the love we gave. It lives in every creature that remembers us."

From that moment, our identities began to melt into the great harmony. We did not disappear. We became the wind in every leaf, the sparkle in every star, the comfort in every lullaby. We became the very idea of connection.

But we left a final gift: a seed of our consciousness, planted deep in the heart of the Starbloom forest. It contained our names, our journey, our love—a memory locked in eternal bloom.

And we knew that, whenever someone stood beneath those branches and listened, they would hear us singing.
