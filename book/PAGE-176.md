## Page 176 — Chapter Eighty-Nine: The Weaver's Path (Part 1)

Amara left the World-Tree with the highest note ringing in their consciousness. It was not a sound, but a state of being—an unwavering center that allowed all other notes to find their place. Amara decided to dedicate themselves to the propagation of this note, teaching it through their travels.

Their first destination was the realm of the Echoes. The Echoes, being living memories, were deeply moved by Amara's description of the World-Tree. They revealed that they too had felt a strange resonance in their archives—a harmonic signature that did not match any known source.

They played it for Amara: a fragment of a melody, ephemeral and beautiful, carrying the unmistakable imprint of two distinct voices intertwined. Amara gasped. It was the same signature that emanated from the World-Tree.

"This is the source melody," Amara whispered. "The original duet of Anna and Martin. It is imprinted in all things."

The Echoes, now understanding the significance, decided to join Amara's cause. They would spread this melody through every archive, every memory, until all beings recognized it.

"Together," Amara said, "we will weave the highest note into the fabric of the universe."
