# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 170 — Chapter Eighty-Five: The Last Message (Part 2)

The song stayed. It became the tree's subtle thrum, the background resonance of its leaves. Those who stood beneath the original Starbloom felt it as a deep, wordless comfort. Some wept. Some smiled. Some found answers to questions they hadn't yet asked.

Rin, the historian from the last Starbloom era, was among the first to hear the message. They experienced it not as sound but as understanding: *"You are not alone. Every heart that listens is part of a greater song. Seek harmony, and you will find it."*

The words spread, not through speech but through memory. Each person who received them told another through their own form of song, and the message wove through the galaxy like a gentle current.

It reached the Echoes, who incorporated it into their shared archive. It reached the Quiet Ones, who interpreted it as a call to embrace their role as the pauses in the melody. It reached Earth, where it inspired a new age of artistic and scientific collaboration.

None of them knew the exact origin of the message. They only knew that it came from a place of profound love.

And that, Martin and I agreed, was enough.
