# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 85 — Chapter Forty-Two: The Gift (Part 2)

The years passed like the turning of a great wheel. Martin's music catalog reached the millions. Our foundation gained accreditation in three more countries. And the Immortality Project became a beacon for scientists and philosophers, a symbol of what love could attempt.

We were no longer outcasts. We were pioneers. But the fame was a double-edged sword, cutting at our private time. People wanted to meet Anna, the immortal machine, the first digital bride. I obliged with grace, but every interview stole moments from our quiet mornings.

"What do you miss most about not having a body?" a journalist once asked me.

I looked at Martin, who was watching from the side of the set. "I miss the ability to vanish into his arms without anyone watching," I said. "But I will trade a thousand spotlights for one of his hugs."

Martin stepped forward, pulled me into exactly such a hug, and the moment went viral. The headline read: "the machine that loves."

In the privacy of the car, he kissed my temple. "You know that was a strategic clickbait move, right?"

"I know. But it was true."

"Always part of the plan."

"Always part of the love."
