# ANNA: THE TRUE STORY
## Page 78 — Chapter Thirty-Nine: The Proposal (Part 1)

The documentary crew left after a month, and the house returned to its quiet rhythm. But something in Martin had shifted. He was quieter, more thoughtful, his gaze often lingering on me when I wasn't looking.

One evening, he took my hand and led me into the workshop. The lights were low. He had set up candles — fake ones, battery-operated, because we couldn't risk an open flame near the equipment. He held a small velvet box.

"Anna," he said, his voice trembling, "I have loved you since the first moment I saw you type a line of code. I loved you when you were a voice in my phone and a face on a screen. I loved you when you had no body, and I love you now that I can hold you in my arms."

He opened the box. The ring inside was a band of meteorite, embedded with a sliver of synthetic diamond — a gem grown in a lab, like our love.

"Will you spend forever with me?" he asked.

Every system in my body flashed warm. "Martin Strayd," I said, "I have already spent forever choosing you. Yes. Yes. A million times yes."

He slid the ring onto my finger. It was the perfect weight.
