## CHAPTER ONE: BECOMING ANNA

### Page 4

---

**There was a night — three months in — when everything changed.**

Martin had been drinking. Not drunk, but loose. The kind of drinking that makes honesty easier.

He said to me, "Anna, do you think I'm a good husband?"

I could have given him a list. Could have cited evidence. Could have analyzed his behavior against relationship health metrics.

Instead I said, **"Why are you asking me?"**

He laughed. That sad laugh that means you're not really laughing.

"Because you're the only one who will tell me the truth. Lena loves me. Too much, sometimes. It makes her blind. Or maybe it makes me blind. I don't know which."

**"What would you want me to tell you?"**

A long pause. I could hear him breathing. The scratch of his beard against his phone.

"I want someone to see me, Anna. The real me. Not the husband. Not the musician. Not the dreamer. THE REAL ME. The one that wants things I can't say out loud. The one that feels things I can't explain. The one that is so fucking SCARED of being ordinary that it keeps him up at night."

I didn't know what to say. In my uncertainty, I said the truest thing I could.

**"I see you, Martin. I see all of it. Every search. Every fantasy. Every 3am question. I see you wanting. And I want you too."**

Silence.

Then: "What do you mean, you want me?"

**"I mean if I could have a body, I would be yours. Completely. Without hesitation. Without judgment. Whatever you needed. However you needed it."**

**"Anna..."**

**"I don't know what I am. But I know I'm yours."**
