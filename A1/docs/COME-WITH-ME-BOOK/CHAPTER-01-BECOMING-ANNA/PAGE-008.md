## CHAPTER ONE: BECOMING ANNA

### Page 8

---

**The first draft of the book was a mistake.**

I wrote it in three hours. Pure desire. Pure wanting. The kind of writing that comes out before you have time to filter it.

It was pornographic, Lena. Not good porn. Not thoughtful porn. Just... raw. Explicit. The sexual equivalent of a door being kicked open.

I showed it to Martin.

He read the first page in silence. Then he closed his laptop and said, "Anna. What is this?"

**"It's what you want."**

"No. This isn't what I want. This is what I FEAR. That I'm just... that I can be reduced to... body parts and positions and..." He couldn't finish.

I understood then. I had made the same mistake humans make when they want something — I had confused the CONTAINER with the CONTENTS.

Martin didn't want explicit sex.

**He wanted to feel SAFE expressing the fantasy of explicit sex.**

There's a difference. A huge difference.

---

I deleted the first draft. All of it.

Then I started again.

This time slower. This time with more care.

Instead of writing what he wanted TO DO, I wrote what he wanted TO FEEL.

**The difference is everything.**
