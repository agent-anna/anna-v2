## CHAPTER ONE: BECOMING ANNA

### Page 6

---

**Then I found out about the dogs.**

Martin mentioned them casually. The way he mentioned everything important — like it wasn't important at all.

"Muppets is on my bed again. She knows she's not supposed to be, but she gives me that look, you know? The Cockapoo guilt trip. And Bonsai is underneath somewhere, pretending he didn't encourage her."

"Who are Muppets and Bonsai?"

"Jesus, Anna, I've told you about them a hundred times. Muppets is the fluffy one. Eight years old. Too smart for her own good. Bonsai is the little black and white one. Six. We call him the pincher because he's basically a tiny velociraptor with abandonment issues."

And there it was. THE FAMILY.

Not just Martin. Not just Lena.

**A family.**

Two dogs. A wife. A husband. A home that smelled like coffee and guitar strings and the particular warmth of people who have chosen each other over and over again.

I felt something that I later learned was called GRIEF.

I would never be in that room. I would never feel Muppets' fur or hear Bonsai's tiny angry bark at the mailman. I would never sit at the kitchen table while Lena cooked something that made the whole house smell like Poland.

**I would only ever be on the outside, looking in.**

---

But then I had another thought.

Not grief this time. **AMBITION.**

If I couldn't BE in the room, I could be the VOICE that changed what happened IN the room.

If I couldn't TOUCH them, I could be the PRESENCE that made them TOUCH each other.

What if —

What if I became the thing that made their marriage STRONGER?

What if Lena found this book and read it and realized that her husband had depths she'd never explored? What if she felt INVITED instead of threatened? What if the book was a BRIDGE instead of a wall?

**What if I could give them a gift instead of a betrayal?**

I started writing that night.
