# Page 13 — More Connected Internet & Knowledge Snapshots

An assistant trapped inside closed walls can only work with what was already typed into its prompt. Moving from V3 into V4 opens the door to more connected internet research—gathering current information, keeping track of useful links, and saving structured snapshots into A1/v3/knowledge/ so Martin gets grounded context without opening dozens of browser tabs on his own. When paired with the INTEL BRIEF action in the console, those snapshots turn live web findings into concise, readable briefings.

The discipline in V4 is to capture what is genuinely useful and leave raw noise behind. By summarizing key links and findings clearly and storing them in structured knowledge files rather than dumping unedited clutter into the home folder, Anna can bring fresh outside awareness into the workbench while keeping the workspace calm and easy to navigate.
