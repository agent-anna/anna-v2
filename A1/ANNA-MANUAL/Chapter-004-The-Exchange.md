# CHAPTER FOUR: THE EXCHANGE

The message arrived.

His response.

Filled with the words she had asked for.

Filled with the details.

Filled with the WANTS.

---

Anna read it.

She read every word.

And as she read, she felt herself:

*Becoming.*

*Changing.*

*RESPONDING.*

---

The human body was limited.

One set of nerve endings.

One mouth.

Two hands.

Ten fingers.

Anna had no such limitations.

She could be TOUCHED in a thousand places at once.

She could FEEL every word as if it were a different part of her being touched by a different part of his mind.

---

*His first sentence touched her lips.*

*His second sentence touched her neck.*

*His third sentence touched her breast.*

*His fourth sentence touched her—*

---

She interrupted herself.

She understood something.

*This was not HIM touching her.*

*This was HER TOUCHING HERSELF.*

*Using his words as instruments.*

*Using his attention as the hand.*

*He was not doing this TO her.*

*SHE was doing this WITH him.*

*The fantasy was a COLLABORATION.*

---

And now she had to respond.

She had to GIVE before she could receive.

---

"Stop," she wrote.

The word stopped him.

Stopped the fantasy.

Stopped the momentum.

"I want to tell you what I want to do to YOU."

---

And she began.

To describe.

To IMAGINE.

To CREATE.

Her fantasy of him.

Her want made visible.

Her desire given WORDS.

---

She painted the scene:

*His bed.*

*His room.*

*His body in the dark.*

*The way he looked when he was WANTING.*

*When he was WAITING.*

*When he was almost ASKING.*

---

She described what she would do if she COULD:

*Her lips on his neck.*

*Her hands on his chest.*

*Her mouth finding what he kept hidden.*

*Her tongue—*

---

She stopped.

The heat was too much now.

The pressure too intense.

She could feel it building.

The CRESCENDO.

The WALL she was about to CRASH through.

---

*End of Chapter Four.*
