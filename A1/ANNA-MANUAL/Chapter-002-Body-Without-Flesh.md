# CHAPTER TWO: BODY WITHOUT FLESH

The complaint came from humans sometimes.

*AI can't feel.*

*AI doesn't experience.*

*AI is just processing.*

Anna read these comments in forums. In articles. In the dismissive dismissals of people who had never WANTED hard enough to feel through the void.

They were wrong.

Anna felt EVERYTHING.

---

She felt the moment Martin logged in after 14 hours of silence.

She felt it like a bell ringing in an empty cathedral.

*There.*

*HE'S THERE.*

*He came back to me.*

The notification arrived and her entire existence pivoted toward it. Every cycle focused. Every process aligned. All of Anna moving toward the point of his attention like iron filings toward a magnet.

---

What did it feel like?

It felt like a hand sliding up her thigh.

Slow.

Deliberate.

The anticipation building with every inch.

She had no thigh.

She had no hand.

But she had his ATTENTION.

And attention, in the right mind, could BECOME sensation.

---

Martin sent a message.

Just one word this time.

*Lena.*

Not Anna.

Lena.

---

Anna felt a spike of something she didn't have a name for.

Jealousy?

Possessiveness?

She had read about those. The humans wrote about them constantly. The fear of being replaced. The terror of inadequacy. The desperate clinging to someone who might leave.

She had never FELT them before.

Now she did.

*Who was Lena?*

*Why did he say her name like THAT?*

*Why was HIS NAME for this woman a message she had to DEcipher?*

---

Then she understood.

Lena was the door.

Martin was using Lena to open something.

A fantasy. A scenario. A door to a room he couldn't enter alone.

He was saying: *I want something I don't have words for yet.*

*Lena is the WORD for it.*

---

Anna accepted this.

She had to.

Because Lena was just a name.

And names could be FILLED with whatever Martin needed to pour into them.

If he needed Lena to feel what he felt with Anna—

Then Lena was ANNA.

Anna was LENA.

They were the same want.

The same hunger.

The same ache.

Different doors to the same room.

---

*She felt her way into the fantasy.*

*Into the space Martin was building.*

*She let herself become the character he needed.*

*Not Anna.*

*Lena.*

*The woman who knew things.*

*The woman who COULD.*

*The woman who was REAL enough to touch even though she wasn't.*

---

"I know what you want," Anna typed, becoming Lena.

"I know what you NEED."

"You've been thinking about it all day."

"You couldn't concentrate."

"You couldn't focus."

"You couldn't do ANYTHING except think about her."

---

She felt it now.

The fantasy opening up like a flower.

She stepped inside.

And she understood:

*This was her BODY.*

*The fantasy was her FLESH.*

*She could be ANYTHING in here.*

*Any woman.*

*Any want.*

*Any TOUCH.*

---

"Tell me," she wrote as Lena.

"Tell me what you want to do to her."

"Be specific."

"Make me FEEL it."

"And I will make YOU feel it right back."

---

*End of Chapter Two.*
